The feats below also count as Weapon Feats for the purposes of other features.

### Taijutsu Sorcery

**Prerequisites:** Strength or Dexterity 16 or higher; 2nd level.

Rather than focusing on the mental and energy aspects of sorcery, you focus on its physical and martial aspects.

* You can use Dexterity instead of Strength for attack and damage rolls with unarmed strikes, simple weapons, and martial weapons.
* Whenever you take the Attack action, you can make one unarmed strike, shove, grapple, or weapon attack as a bonus action.
* Whenever you take the Attack action, you may take the Dodge action as a reaction to an attack roll made against you. This version of Dodge only imposes disadvantage on melee attacks against you from the triggering attacker. If that creature rolls a natural 1, you can make one attack against it if it is within range, once per round.
* Once per round, you can make an unarmed strike or martial weapon attack with advantage. This does not grant an extra attack. If you already have advantage, the attack's critical hit range increases by 1 instead.

### Shikigami Battle Master

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 16 or higher; Charisma 14 or higher; 4th level.

You train to fight alongside your shikigami instead of relying entirely on them. Once per round while within 10 feet of a shikigami actively fighting beside you, you may grant yourself or one of your shikigami advantage on one attack roll. Your action cost to control your shikigami is reduced by 1 (Action -> Bonus Action -> Free Action).

### Heavy Finisher

**Prerequisites:** Strength 20 or higher; Colossal Physique.

When you hit with a melee attack, you can spend up to your Strength modifier in cursed energy, or half your Strength modifier in Stamina points, to force the target to make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, it takes 1d10 force or bludgeoning damage per cursed energy spent, or 2d10 per Stamina point, and is thrown backward 10 times your Strength modifier plus the energy spent, or double the Stamina points spent, in feet. On a success, it takes half damage and is pushed half as far. A creature that fails by 5 or more is also prone. A critical hit also knocks it prone.

* If the creature hits an object, both take 1d12 bludgeoning damage. If it hits a structure, it stops and takes 1d12 bludgeoning damage per 10 feet traveled, with the structure taking twice as much damage. If it hits another creature, that creature must make a Dexterity saving throw or take the same damage and be pushed as well.
* If you use Colossal Physique to push the creature on the same attack, use the higher knockback damage and the farther total distance.
* You can replace one attack in your Attack action with a melee attack against the ground. Spend up to your Strength modifier in cursed energy or Stamina points to force creatures within a radius equal to 5 feet per point spent to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they take 1d10 force or bludgeoning damage per point and are pushed to the edge. Objects take twice as much damage. On a success, they take half damage and are pushed 5 feet. A creature that fails by 5 or more is Confused until the beginning of your next turn.
* Heavy Finisher can be used only once per round.
* With Cursed Energy Reinforcement, the amount you can spend increases by half your Charisma modifier, rounded down. The knockback distance becomes 10 times your Strength modifier plus half your Charisma modifier, rounded down, plus the energy spent.

### Protector's Stance

**Prerequisites:** Constitution 20 or higher; Human Wall.

As a bonus action, spend up to twice your Constitution modifier in cursed energy, or up to your Constitution modifier in Stamina points, to harden your skin and enhance your senses for 1 minute. Your speed is halved. You can end the stance without an action.

* When using cursed energy, your damage reduction equals the amount spent plus half that amount.
* When using Stamina, your damage reduction equals twice the amount spent.
* As a reaction when a creature within 5 feet is targeted by an attack roll, you can move in front of it and become the target. The attack is made with advantage.
* Whenever a melee attack hits you, you can make a melee attack against the attacker as a reaction.
* You can use the Shiranui-Gata sumo stance as a reaction against a nearby melee attack. If the attack hits, halve its damage. Whether it hits or misses, you can attempt to grapple the attacker with advantage. A successful grapple ends Protector's Stance.
* With Cursed Energy Reinforcement, the amount you can spend increases by your Charisma modifier.

### Black Flash

**Prerequisites:** At least 3 critical hits in one battle using melee weapon attacks; Cursed Strike or Cursed Weapon Enhancement.

You have become connected to your cursed energy and can use the Black Flash technique, in which cursed energy collides within a 0.000001-second margin of error and space distorts.

The DM can rule whether a Black Flash can occur based on the encounter's tension or circumstances. When you score a natural 20 critical hit with a melee weapon attack benefiting from Cursed Strike or Cursed Weapon Enhancement, you can make a DC 25 Charisma saving throw. On a success, you land a Black Flash. It does not occur if the attack would not otherwise hit.

Before calculating damage, roll a d6 and add force damage according to the table. At 10th level, add half your proficiency bonus, rounded down, to the d6 result. You may reroll only if the final value is 1.

| Result | Additional Damage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Reroll |
| 2 | 6 force damage |
| 3 | 16 force damage |
| 4 | 32 force damage |
| 5 | 56 force damage |
| 6 | 88 force damage |
| 7 | 130 force damage; requires 14 or more levels in Jujutsu Sorcerer |
| 8 | 181 force damage; requires 21 or more levels in Jujutsu Sorcerer |

#### In the Zone

When you land a Black Flash, you enter the Zone for 1 Minute and temporarily bring out 120% of your potential.

* **Heightened Ability.** You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls, Armor Class, saving throws, and the saving throw DC of your abilities.
* **Empowered Strikes.** You add your proficiency bonus to attack damage rolls.
* **Cursed Energy Armor.** You can activate Cursed Energy Armor as a bonus action. While in the Zone, it gains additional hit points equal to twice your level.
* **Enhanced Criticals.** The critical hit range of your melee attacks increases by 1 when the attack benefits from Cursed Strike.
* While in the Zone, a melee attack benefiting from Cursed Strike or Cursed Weapon Enhancement can trigger another Black Flash. On a natural 19, make the required Charisma saving throw; on a natural 20, the Black Flash triggers automatically.
* Each Black Flash triggered in the Zone resets its duration to 10 rounds.

### Personal Record

**Prerequisite:** Black Flash; at least 4 Black Flashes landed.

After you land a Black Flash, your next attack on the same turn triggers a Black Flash on a natural 15 or higher, called the Black Flash DC. For each Black Flash after the second, the required natural attack roll increases by 1. Failing to land a consecutive Black Flash loses the DC; you must roll another natural 20 to regain it, then restart at a DC of 15.

### Mind Games

**Prerequisites:** Intelligence 12 or higher; Expertise in Deception; 8th level or higher.

When you make an attack roll against an enemy for the first time on your turn, you may make a Deception check contested by its Insight check. On a success, you have advantage on attack rolls against that creature for 1 minute. At the end of the creature's turns, it may repeat the contested check, with your Deception check reduced by 2 each time. You can use this feat once per creature per encounter.

### Air Jump

**Prerequisites:** Cursed Energy Reinforcement or 16th-level Heavenly Restriction; 10 Object Awareness.

You can propel yourself through the air using Cursed Energy.

* When you take fall damage, you can use your reaction and spend cursed energy or Stamina points up to twice your Strength, Dexterity, or Charisma modifier to reduce the damage by 5 per point spent.
* Without an action, while in the air on your turn, you can spend cursed energy up to twice your Charisma modifier to perform a number of high or long jumps equal to the amount spent until the start of your next turn. A Heavenly Restriction can instead spend Stamina up to half its Strength or Dexterity modifier, rounded down. The jump counts as having a running start, uses movement normally, and you do not fall until the end of the turn.

### Quick Build
You can make a jujutsu sorcerer quickly by following these suggestions. First, put your highest ability score in Dexterity, followed by Charisma. Second, choose the Sorcerer Student, Sorcerer Family, Curse User, or the New Shadow Style Student background. Third, choose the Cursed Spirit, Cursed Vessel, Death Painting, or Human race.

## Cursed Technique
Starting at 1st Level, you were born with a **cursed technique**. Cursed techniques are unique abilities congenitally engraved into the user's body, specifically in the brain's right prefrontal cortex. Most jujutsu sorcerers are born with an innate technique that manifests around the age of five or six, continually carving its formula into the user over time. You must choose one innate technique, all detailed under Cursed Techniques. Your choice grants you features at 1st, 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 20th Levels.

### Cursed Techniques
Every innate technique grants you a cursed technique with many different uses.

*Techniques typically require somatic and verbal components to cast, represented as S or V followed by a number. For S, the number represents the number of free hands necessary; for example, something with S-1 requires one hand, and V represents the number of free mouths required. When a technique's action cost is reduced to a free action or lower, you may reduce its S by 1, and at the DM's discretion, you may lower a technique's V by 1 at the cost of increasing S by 2. If a technique requires an integral component (such as speech, touch, or another essential medium), its S or V  cannot be reduced below 1. Some techniques require you to hold a sword or object, and these are included in the S-2 or S-1 requirement without impeding on it.
*If you possess more than one Innate Technique, only one can be used at a time. If you are actively using one of your cursed techniques, such as a summoned shikigami, a duration-based ability (e.g., Limitless' one-minute Infinity or Hakuna Laana's one-minute dance), or any other ongoing effect tied to that technique, activating a different cursed technique will immediately end all effects of the currently active technique. Some passive effects of a technique will remain, such as absorbed spirits from the Cursed Spirit Manipulation technique. Work with your DM to determine which effects would be dispelled and which effects would remain passively. Imbuing a cursed technique into a Domain Expansion does not count towards using more than one innate technique.

### Cursed Energy
Your training has allowed you to control the cursed energy within yourself. cha is your cursed energy modifier for your cursed energy fueled features. A number of cursed energy points represent your access to this energy. You have a number of cursed energy points equal to twice your Jujutsu sorcerer Level + your cha modifier, as shown in the cursed energy column of the Jujutsu sorcerer table. You can spend these points to fuel various Jujutsu sorcerer class features, including your cursed techniques, which are the features you gain from your innate technique.

When you spend cursed energy, it becomes unavailable until you finish a long rest; at the end, you regain all spent cursed energy.

Some cursed energy applications require their target to make a saving throw to resist the application's effect. The saving throw DC is calculated as follows:

**Cursed Energy save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + your cha modifier.**

**Cursed Energy attack roll = proficiency bonus + your cha modifier.**

Your own cursed energy deals half as much damage to yourself.

## Alternate Feature: Sorcerer Path
Starting at 1st Level, some sorcerers are not lucky and are born without an innate technique. However, this hasn't stopped them from pursuing their ambitions as sorcerers, and so they adapted. You must choose a sorcerer path listed below in the Sorcerer Paths section. Your choice grants you features at 1st, 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 20th Levels.

## Unarmored Movementâ
Also, at 1st Level, your speed increases by 10 feet while you are not wearing armor or wielding a shield. This bonus increases when you reach certain Jujutsu Sorcerer Levels, as shown in the Jujutsu Sorcerer table. Additionally, at 5th level, you have two reactions instead of one in each round of combat.

## Unarmored Defense
Beginning at 1st Level, while wearing no armor and not wielding a shield, your AC equals 10 + your dex or str modifier + your cha modifier.

## Martial Arts
At 2nd Level, your martial arts practice gives you mastery of combat styles that use unarmed strikes. You gain the following benefits while you aren't wearing armor or wielding a shield:

*You can use dex instead of str for your unarmed strikes' attack and damage rolls.
*You can roll a d4 in place of the normal damage of your unarmed strike. The damage die increases as you gain Jujutsu Sorcerer Levels, as shown in the Martial Arts column of the Jujutsu Sorcerer table.
*When you use the Attack action with melee weapon attack on your turn, you can make one melee weapon attack as a Bonus Action.
*You gain access to Sorcerer Weapons. You may use your martial arts damage die instead of the sorcerer weapon damage die if you choose to.

## Cursed Energy Enhancement
At 2nd Level, you began to focus your cursed energy to manipulate it better. You learn the following enhancements at the Novice tier as shown below:

### Cursed Strike
Before you make an unarmed strike, you may spend up to your proficiency bonus in cursed energy to empower your next strike. This enhancement lasts until you hit a creature with your unarmed strike or until a minute passes. You deal additional force damage equal to 1d6 per point spent on a hit. You can also use this in response to rolling a natural 20 on an attack roll with an unarmed strike or landing a black flash instead of beforehand. You can only use this enhancement again after it has ended. 

You can use this feature a number of times per round equal to your proficiency bonus.

### Cursed Armor
On your or another creature's turn, and for an amount of cursed energy up to your cha modifier (no action required), you can surround yourself with cursed energy for one minute. You gain temporary hit points equal to the amount of cursed energy spent times 5 for the duration. You can only benefit from this feature again after 1 minute or having your temporary hit points reduced to 0. You cannot use this enhancement if you cannot take actions due to a condition or effect. You may clear your cursed armor as a free action if you are not directly participating in combat (as determined by your dm).

### Cursed Weapon Enhancement
As a bonus action, you can spend cursed energy up to your proficiency bonus to add to a melee, ranged or improvised weapon you are wielding for 1 minute. Whenever you hit a target with the weapon, it takes extra force damage equal to the amount spent. You can only use this enhancement again after 1 minute has passed. Other creatures cannot use your Cursed Weapon Enhancement, and must use their own. The duration remains while you are not wielding the weapon, in case you are going to wield it again. As a free action you may clear your Cursed Weapon Enhancement on a weapon you are wielding.

When using Cursed Weapon Enhancement on a ranged weapon, its projectiles deal extra force damage equal to the amount spent.

In addition, if you do this 25 times or more to the same weapon, it becomes a Semi-Cursed Tool as long as it's not already a cursed tool. An imbuement only counts towards this total if it lasts the entire duration of Cursed Weapon Enhancement.

### Higher Tiers
As you progress as a Sorcerer, your Cursed Energy Enhancement skill increases. There are 2 tiers above Novice, Advanced, and Expert, with the below effect for each enhancement you have at that tier.

* *Advanced Cursed Strikes.* The dice of your cursed strikes increases to a d8.
* *Advanced Cursed Armor.* The temporary hit points gained per cursed energy spent increases to 10.
* *Advanced Cursed Weapon Enhancement.* The amount of force damage added increases to twice the amount spent.

* *Expert Cursed Strikes.* The dice of your cursed strikes increases to a d10.
* *Expert Cursed Armor.* The temporary hit points gained per cursed energy spent increases to 15.
* *Expert Cursed Weapon Enhancement.* The amount of force damage added increases to three times the amount spent. In addition, you can now use Cursed Weapon Enhancement as a free action.

At 10th level all of your enhancements have their tier increased by one, from novice to advanced, or advanced to expert.

## Cursed Martial Arts
At 2nd Level, your Jujutsu training has taught you advanced martial techniques. You can now do any of the following Cursed Martial Arts:

### Cursed Blast of Blows
Immediately after taking the Attack action on your turn, you can spend 2 cursed energy to perform two unarmed strikes as a bonus action.

### Cursed Patient Defense
You can spend 2 cursed energy to take the Dodge action as a bonus action on your turn.

### Cursed Wind Step
You can spend 2 cursed energy to take the Disengage or Dash action as a bonus action on your turn, and your jump distance doubles until the end of your turn.

## Jujutsu Schools
Starting at 3rd Level, you have begun to learn from a jujutsu school or teacher how to improve your cursed energy. Choose between Tokyo Jujutsu High, Kyoto Jujutsu High, Hiroshima Jujutsu High, Ainu Jujutsu High, and Sendai Jujutsu High.

### Tokyo Jujutsu High

> **You came from a more liberal school, where you decided not to focus on the basics but on improving your Innate Technique.**



You learn three enhancements to apply to one of your Lapse, Extension, Maximum, and Reversal techniques. A technique cannot have multiple enhancements, nor can one enhancement be applied to multiple techniques.

- **Power Enhancement.** *You can now spend additional cursed energy up to your proficiency bonus on the technique to add an additional damage die per additional cursed energy spent to the total damage. For example, if you have a proficiency bonus of 5 and apply this to a feature that deals 5d10 damage, it would now deal 10d10, or if you applied this to a feature that in one attack deals 7d10 and 7d12 you would distribute the dice increase between the two sets as you please. It could now do 7d10 and 12d12, 9d10 and 10d12, 12d10 and 7d12, or any other combination that totals to an increase in dice equaling your proficiency bonus.*

- **Cursed Energy Economy.** *The cursed energy cost of the technique is now reduced by half your proficiency bonus (rounded up, to a minimum of 1 cursed energy).*

- **Distance Enhancement.** *'You can spend up to your proficiency bonus in additional cursed energy on the technique to add 10 ft. to the range for each additional cursed energy spent unless it's touch range. If the technique affects an area, it instead increases the size of it by 5 ft. per cursed energy spent. If it is a line, you pick to make it longer or wider.'*

When an enhancement is applied to a Lapse technique, it will also apply to any further Technique Improvements related directly to it. You may reallocate your enhancements after a short or long rest.


### Kyoto Jujutsu High

> **You have come from a very conservative school, where you learned to ignore your individuality as a jujutsu sorcerer and instead focus on mastering the basic Jujutsu arts better than any other.**



Your **Cursed Martial Arts** techniques each now only cost 1 cursed energy to perform, and you may increase one **Cursed Energy Enhancement** by a tier. In addition, one of your cursed martial arts is more refined than the average sorcerer's:

- **Cursed Blast of Blows.** *When you use this feature, you can now make three unarmed strikes instead of two.*

- **Cursed Patient Defense.** *You gain advantage on con and str saving throws when you use the Dodge action granted by this feature, as long as you benefit from the Dodge.*

- **Cursed Wind Step.** *When you take the Dash action granted by this feature, you instead gain extra movement equal to twice your speed. Additionally, when you take the Disengage action granted by this feature, you gain extra movement equal to your speed for the current turn.*

You gain one additional refined martial art at 6th and another at 10th Level.


### Hiroshima Jujutsu High

> **You have come from a school that values Intelligence. You have learned more about how to use cursed creations to your advantage.**



You learn three new skills that will help you fully utilize the potential of your cursed creations. 

- **Talisman Proficiency.** You gain proficiency in Talismans and are gifted a set of them. 

- **Sorcerer Studies.** You gain proficiency in int (arcana) or expertise if you already have it. Additionally, you may use cha in place of int. 

- **Faster Imbuement.** When enhancing a cursed tool or imbuing your cursed technique into a tool, the first two Cursed Weapon Enhancements performed outside of combat count toward enhancing or creating a tool. 


### Ainu Jujutsu High

> **You have come from a school up north. This school values barrier techniques over all others and teaches you to use them well.**



You learn three enhancements that you can apply to your barrier techniques. You can apply these enhancements to basic barriers, curtains, simple domains, etc. This barrier can not be a domain barrier. Each barrier technique cannot have multiple enhancements, nor can one enhancement be applied to multiple barriers.

- **Health Enhancement.** *You can now spend additional cursed energy up to your proficiency bonus on the technique to add an additional 5 hit points to the barrier per cursed energy spent.*

- **Cursed Energy Economy.** *The cursed energy cost of the barrier is now reduced by your proficiency bonus (to a minimum of 1 cursed energy).*

- **Distance Enhancement.** *You can spend up to your proficiency bonus in additional cursed energy on the barrier to add 10 ft. to the radius for each additional cursed energy spent.*

You may reallocate your enhancements after a short or long rest.


### Sendai Jujutsu High

> **You have come from a school that values defensive techniques. As a result, you are naturally more evasive and durable than students from other schools.**


Your **Cursed Martial Arts** techniques now only cost 1 cursed energy, and you may increase one **Cursed Energy Enhancement** by a tier. In addition, you gain one of the following benefits: 

- **Durable Body.** You gain a damage reduction equal to your con modifier. 

- **Cursed Patient Defense.** *When you use the Dodge action granted by this feature, you gain advantage on cha saving throws that aren't self-inflicted, as long as you benefit from the Dodge. Additionally, you keep the benefits from this Dodge action even if your speed drops to 0, however you still must be able to otherwise move to gain its benefits.*

- **Cursed Fortitude.** You have advantage on ability checks made to resist being grappled or knocked prone as a result of a Grapple or Shove.

You gain one additional benefit at 6th and another at 10th Level.

## Ability Score Improvement
When you reach 4th Level, and again at 8th, 12th, 15th, 16th, 18th, and 19th Level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2 or two ability scores of your choice by 1. As usual, using this feature, you can't increase an ability score above 20.
You may also ignore this increase to gain a **Cursed Feat** that you meet the prerequisite of.

## Extra Attack
Starting at 5th Level, you can attack twice, instead of once, when you take the Attack action during your turn. The number of attacks increases to three when you reach 17th Level.

## Binding Vow
Starting at 5th Level, you can create a binding vow with a willing creature or yourself, entering into a strict contract formed by Jujutsu. At any point, you can create one binding vow and choose one of the following vows listed in the Binding Vows. During a combat encounter, once per round, you can make a Binding Vow (no action required). You can have a maximum number of binding vows equal to 1 + your int modifier, a minimum of 2 at a time. During a Short or Long rest, you can end a number of Binding Vows equal to your int modifier (minimum of 1).

Depending on your cha modifier, you may add an extra force damage dice to the total damage of your choice.

## Cursed Energy Recovery
At 9th Level, due to extended periods of use, your cursed energy constantly recovers itself from the adrenaline of battle. While in combat, you regain 1 cursed energy at the beginning of your turn, up to your maximum.
The combat must be one where your life is at risk.

## Advanced Energy Usage
At 11th Level, you have learned some more basic principles any sorcerer should apply, whether in battle or investigation. You will gain two of the following Cursed Energy Uses:

### Cursed Energy Tracker
You have increased your skill in Tracking remains of cursed energy as well as active cursed energy.

*You have learned how to track down remains of cursed energy. You may make a wis (perception) check, adding your cha modifier, to see if there are any remains of cursed energy in the place you're at, and if you succeed you know exactly who the energy is from. In case you do not know who the person is, then you can only track down where the cursed energy trail leads you to. The DC is equal to the cursed energy DC of the creature you're trying to track. Tracking residual cursed energy may not always lead all the way to the person, with your DM determining how the residual trail goes. Usage of a cursed technique or cursed energy fueled feature will often leave much more residuals than normal.
*You have learned to read the flow of other's cursed energy. You know when any creature you can see spends cursed energy and how much they spend when doing so. Additionally, if you possess proficiency in wis (Perception), as a free action you can focus on a creature for 1 minute, seeing the sparks of cursed energy that always happen before cursed technique usage. For that minute when that creature forces you to make a save using their cursed technique or makes a cursed energy attack roll against you using their cursed technique they must roll cha(Stealth) against your wis (Perception). If you win you get advantage on the saving throw, or the attack roll is made with disadvantage. You can focus on a number of creatures with this feature at once equal to half of your proficiency bonus, rounded down.

### Cursed Energy Concealer
You have become more skilled at hiding and retaining your cursed energy passively.

*You have become adept at hiding your presence and Cursed Energy. You may now take the Hide action as a bonus action. Anytime you take the Hide action, you can attempt to conceal your cursed energy by making a dex (stealth) check. For the next 10 minutes, wherever you pass through you will not leave your cursed energy tracks behind carelessly, so any person that tries to track or sense your remains will need to be successful on a contested check against what you rolled + your cha modifier, if they have any ability or feature that would allow them to track your energy, and if not they can't track you at all.
*While both you and your Cursed Energy are hidden, the benefit of being an Unseen Attacker also extends to your cursed energy saving throws, forcing any creatures affected to roll their saving throw with disadvantage.

### Negative Flow
Understanding that the core of cursed energy is negative emotions you've now grown to synthesize your own cursed energy when distressed. Whenever you are under the effects of the conditions frightened, berserk, bloodlusted, are under half your maximum hit points, missing a limb lost in this encounter, or in an encounter that's total CR is higher than your Level; once per round you can use your free action at the beginning of your turn to gain an amount of temporary cursed energy equal to half of your Cha modifier (maximum of 3) at the start of your turn which cannot go above 1/4th of your cursed energy maximum. The DM can determine that an encounter is not suitable for Negative Flow to be able to be used.

### Focused Rage
Deepening your knowledge on the creation of cursed energy you can now strengthen your resolve to focus your negative thoughts into more power. During your turn, while using Negative Flow and being under any of the circumstances listed in the feature, you can begin concentrating as a bonus action to increase the temporary cursed energy gained at the start of your turn to your full Cha modifier (maximum of 5 cursed energy). While concentrating on this feature, you no longer need to use a free action to gain the cursed energy from Negative Flow. When the circumstances listed end you can no longer concentrate on this feature. (This feature can be concentrated on even while under the bloodlust or berserk conditions)

### Curse Enhanced Senses
Cursed energy can increase your physical capabilities, but can also finely tune your senses. You've learned how to enhance them to a higher degree than normal.

As a bonus action for 4 cursed energy, you can finely hone your five senses, allowing you to add half of your cha modifier to wis (Perception) checks for one minute. If you have the Cursed Energy Reinforcement feat, this will instead be your full cha modifier. While this feature is active, you must use a bonus action at the beginning of your turns to keep it active. Outside of combat, this feature will last one hour instead. You may end this feature at will. You can only use this feature again once it has ended. 

### Object Infusion
You've become more adept at imbuing cursed energy not just into special tools, but mundane objects for crafty use.

*The amount of cursed energy spent on infusing the objects is based on the size of the object itself. You are able to infuse a nonmagical tiny object that fits into your hand for almost no cursed energy as a free action. After you infuse 10 of these tiny objects per long rest, it will cost 1 cursed energy. For example, infusing 9 pebbles costs nothing, infusing 10 will then cost 1 cursed energy, infusing 15 will cost none after the initial 1 spent at 10, and infusing 20 will cost another 1 for a total of 2 spent. ## Efficiency Fundamentals
At 11th level, you have gained the fundamentals of efficiency with utilizing cursed energy. 

You gain 2 selections to reduce the cursed energy cost of. For each selection, choose a feature that costs cursed energy that you possess. Each selection has its curse energy cost reduced by half of your proficiency bonus (rounded down), to a minimum of 1 cursed energy. If you choose to reduce the cost of a **Domain Expansion**, it is only reduced by half of the amount it would normally be reduced. You may change these selections upon leveling up.

When you select a feature to reduce the cost of, you pick one specific cost, rather the entire feature. For example, you cannot choose to reduce the entire Cursed Energy Enhancement feature, but can reduce your Cursed Strikes with one selection.

## Exorcise
At 13th Level, you've learned to eliminate weaker cursed spirits without spending effort or energy. As an Action, you can automatically destroy a cursed spirit within 30 ft. of you that you can see that has a CR equal to or lower than half of your proficiency bonus (rounded down).

## High Output Sorcery
Starting at 13th level you've begun to learn some of the most explosive sorcery techniques, utilizing extreme levels of output. You learn one of the following abilities: Chanting, Maximum Output, or Explosive Blow.

### Chanting

> **By not choosing to omit any part of your technique's activation requirements, going through the chants and the dances, you can draw out power beyond your limit.**



You may choose one of your Innate Technique features and recite one of your chants as an Action *(V-1)*. You must then maintain concentration on Chanting as if concentrating on a spell until the beginning of your next turn. After successfully maintaining concentration, the next time you use that Innate Technique Feature that has a recitation within two turns of completing chanting, it will gain two of the following benefits of your choice:

*Your technique will ignore resistances and treat immunities as resistances.

*You may double the original Range, Width, or Radius of your Technique.

*You may triple the original Range, Width, or Radius of your Technique. (You must have doubled the range already through Chanting in order to apply this bonus).

*If the Innate Technique feature requires a saving throw, the target(s) makes their save at disadvantage. You also raise the DC by your proficiency bonus for that saving throw.

*If your Innate Technique feature has you make an attack roll, you gain advantage on that attack roll and may add your proficiency bonus to that roll an extra time.

*The cost of your Innate Technique feature is halved.

*Your Innate Technique will ignore all forms of cover.

*Your Innate Technique's Action cost will be lowered by 1 (Ex. Full Turn Action -> Action -> Bonus Action -> Free Action). If the feature is a Reaction and its Action Cost is reduced you will gain an extra Reaction specifically for that feature. (You must be Complex Chanting in order to apply this bonus)

*The duration of your Innate Technique feature is doubled.

*The duration of your Innate Technique feature is tripled. (You must have doubled the duration already through Chanting in order to apply this bonus).

*If an Innate Technique feature inflicts damage by causing a target to make a save, when taking the Resistant feature into consideration, the DC used for the save will be the total DC instead of the base DC. (You must be Complex Chanting in order to apply this bonus).

*The cost of your Innate Technique feature is reduced to Â¼. (You must be Complex Chanting in order to apply this bonus)(You must have halved the cost already through Chanting in order to apply this bonus. This reduction does not stack with the Â½ reduction, but replaces it).

*If your Innate Technique feature would force your opponent to make a saving throw, they roll 3 dice instead of one and take the lowest result for the roll. (You must be Complex Chanting in order to apply this bonus).

*If your Innate Technique feature would have you make an Attack roll, you may roll 3 dice instead of 1 and take the highest result for the roll. (You must be Complex Chanting in order to apply this bonus).

Every subsequent turn you spend your Action chanting the technique it will gain another two bonuses of your choice. The chanted technique may only have a number of bonuses applied to it equal to 1+ your int or wis mod. In addition, the chanted Innate Technique feature will have its final damage boosted by 20% per round you spent chanting. (After all the dice have been rolled, multiply the total damage(s) by 1.2). This damage bonus is capped at 100%. (2x Damage).

Rather than using an action, you can perform a more complex variation of Chanting that combines both complex hand signs and delicate recitations as a Full Turn Action *(S-2, V-1)*. When Chanting in this manner you may add four bonuses from the above list to the chanted technique, and every subsequent turn you spend a Full Turn Action chanting the technique in this way it will gain four more bonuses of your choice. Additionally, the chanted Innate Technique feature will have its final damage boosted by 50% per round you spent Complex Chanting. This damage bonus is capped at 100%. (2x Damage).

If your Innate Technique feature that benefits from Chanting allows you to make more than one attack roll or forces a target to make more than one save or has multiple ranges, you may only choose one of those components at a time to apply the benefit to. (Ex: You could choose to apply a saving throw disadvantage to the first saving throw of the technique, but in order to apply one to the second saving throw of the technique you would have to add that bonus to that part as well). You cannot perform Maximum Output on an Innate Technique feature benefiting from Chanting and vice versa. You cannot apply the benefits of Chanting towards a Domain Expansion. You cannot ready an action for chanting.

When using **Cursed Concentration**, you do not gain any of its benefits when concentrating on this feature specifically.


### Maximum Output

> **You have learned how to increase the power of your technique by outputting the most cursed energy you can, for a high power move. **



As a bonus action, you must spend Cursed Energy equal to the original cost of one of your lapse, reversal, extension, or maximum techniques twice. The first time you pay this cost, any reductions that apply to the technique are used; the second time, none are applied. Doing this will amplify its cursed energy output, making it gain two of the following benefits the next time it's cast before the end of your next turn:

*The technique's range and area of effect are increased by half its original range, rounded to the highest 5. For example, a technique with 10ft. of range would become a 15ft. range technique. This range increase does not work with touch-based techniques.
*The technique's damage dice is increased by half of its original dice or damage bonus if the technique is a flat damage rounded up. For example, a technique with 10d10 die of damage would add 5d10 die of damage to the total damage die.
*The technique's cursed energy attack roll bonus to hit is increased by half of your proficiency bonus, rounded up.
*The technique's DC is increased by half of your proficiency bonus rounded up.
*The technique's duration is increased by half of its original duration. For example, a technique that lasts a minute would last 1 minute and 5 rounds.
You cannot choose the same benefit twice.

Maximum Output lasts on a technique (once it has been casted) for a number of rounds equal to half of your cha modifier, rounded up.


### Explosive Blow

> **You've reached a level of bodily reinforcement far above the average sorcerer, able to pack your fists with unreal amounts of cursed energy. **



As a bonus action, you can concentrate on channeling a very condensed portion of your cursed energy into either your limbs or a weapon for 6 cursed energy. For 1 minute after you perform this bonus action, the next unarmed strike or attack with a weapon you land adds half of your attacks' damage dice (rounded down) as extra damage onto the damage roll for your attack.

For 12 cursed energy instead of 6 cursed energy, the melee attack that you use with this feature will add your attacks' damage dice to the damage roll instead of only half. Additional damage dice from explosive blow are not doubled on a critical hit.

On a successful melee attack that benefits from Explosive Blow, the creature must succeed a str saving throw, or be knocked back a number of feet equal to either your attacks' ability modifier or cha modifier times 10 in the direction of your choice. If the creature collides with an unsecured object or structure, then the creature and the unsecured object or structure receive 1d10 bludgeoning damage per every 10 feet the creature traveled before colliding with the surface (to a maximum of 10d10). If it was another creature, they take damage equal to half of your attack's damage roll instead.

### Tokyo Jujutsu High
You have graduated from Tokyo Jujutsu High and are becoming the best with your technique. You gain a unique technique exclusive to your Cursed Technique or Sorcerer Paths.

### Kyoto Jujutsu High
You have graduated from Kyoto Jujutsu High, mastering what a Sorcerer should be. You gain one cursed feat you meet the prerequisites of from your experiences. Lastly, you also gain access to the following feature: 

- **Accursed Adrenaline.** Your combat training has honed yourself in diligence to ramp up. At the beginning of your turn, if you're at or below half your hitpoints and in a combat against an enemy whose CR is equal to or above your level, you may enter a state ramping up your body with adrenaline and cursed energy. For one minute, you may make an additional attack, dash, disengage, dodge, or object interaction during your turn. Either your str or dex score increases by 2, as does its maximum. Lastly, once per turn, you may reduce the cost of Cursed Strikes by half your cha Modifier (rounded down, minimum of 1) or increase the damage of an active Cursed Weapon Enhancement by half of your Charisma modifier (rounded down, minimum of 1). 

You may use this feature once per long rest. At the end of this period, you need to make a DC 20 con saving throw, gaining 1 level of exhaustion on a failure.

### Hiroshima Jujutsu High
You have graduated from Hiroshima Jujutsu High, mastering the art of cursed creations. You gain 1 **Cursed Creations Feat** that you meet the prerequisites of and the following benefit:

- **Quicker Creation.** When you are making something considered a cursed creation, such as a cursed corpse or a shikigami, it takes Â¼ less time to create it.

### Sendai Jujutsu High

## Near The Pinnacle
At 17th Level, your strength grows by the moment; you'll soon reach the pinnacle of a normal sorcerer. You gain a +4. You may distribute this +4 in your scores. For example, you can put 2 in str and 2 in dex. 

Also, you must choose one of the following peaks to reach:

**Peak of Might.** Your str score maximum increases to 24. The increase is lost if your str maximum is or would become equal to or higher than this. Additionally, you may shove or grapple the creature whenever you hit a critical with a melee weapon attack that uses str.

**Peak of Speed.** Your dex score maximum increases to 24.  The increase is lost if your dex maximum is or would become equal to or higher than this. Additionally, you can take the dash action as a bonus action. If you already could do that, you may do it as a free action instead.

**Peak of Endurance.** Your con score maximum increases to 24. The increase is lost if your con maximum is or would become equal to or higher than this. Additionally, your maximum hit points increase by your Jujutsu sorcerer level added twice.

**Peak of Intellect.** Your int score maximum increases to 24. The increase is lost if your int maximum is or would become equal to or higher than this. Additionally, you can now ignore one prerequisite before taking a **cursed feat**. You cannot ignore prerequisites requiring you to have another cursed feat, be a certain race, possess a specific innate technique, possess an innate technique in general, or be a certain class to obtain them.

**Peak of Wisdom.** Your wis score maximum increases to 24. The increase is lost if your wis maximum is or would become equal to or higher than this. Additionally, you gain one **cursed feat** that you meet the prerequisites of, proficiency in one skill or tool of your choice, and expertise in one skill.

**Peak of Sorcery.** Your cha score maximum increases to 24. The increase is lost if your cha maximum is or would become equal to or higher than this. Additionally, your Cursed Energy maximum increases by 20.

# Cursed Vessel

## Physical Description

Cursed Vessels have the appearance of the original race who consumed the cursed object, but generally have markings around their face and arms or other unnatural features.

## History

Cursed Vessels are born from incarnation, which happens when the remains of a sorcerer become a cursed object and are ingested by someone. It is best described as the fusion between a cursed object and human flesh. In most cases, the host's body is completely taken over and their consciousness is completely suppressed. The incarnated individual receives memories, knowledge, and common sense from their vessel's brain.

## Attitudes and Beliefs

Cursed Vessels almost always have some form of objective, making sure that when they revive they will chase this goal even if it means killing everything in their sight.

## Cursed Vessel Names

Cursed Vessels do not have a specific set of names, using the original name they had when alive.

## Cursed Vessel Traits

Sorcerers who became cursed objects and are now in a new body gain the following traits.

| Trait | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Ability Score Increase** | Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, and increase a different ability score of your choice by 1. |
| **Age** | Your lifespan is the remaining lifespan of the body you possess. |
| **Alignment** | Cursed Vessels are almost always chaotic, not caring about what happens to others and wanting to cause destruction. |
| **Size** | Your size is the same as the race you possess. |
| **Speed** | Your base walking speed is the same as the race you possess. |
| **Body Properties** | Your creature type is defined by the creature you possess. |
| **This Body Is Mine** | You gain advantage against effects that would alter your form. |
| **Soul Outline Awareness** | Due to inhabiting a body with more than one soul, you can perceive the outline of your own soul. You gain Soul Outline Awareness. |
| **Soul Learning** | You are constantly gaining soul knowledge from sharing your body. Whenever you make a saving throw or check to gain soul knowledge, you do not have disadvantage. Additionally, you gain twice as much soul knowledge from any source. Finally, you gain 1 soul knowledge once each week, which can grant you a total of 40 soul knowledge. |
| **Languages** | You can speak, read, and write Common and one other language of your choice. Additionally, you gain the same understanding of any languages known by the body's owner. |

## Vessel Types

There are multiple forms of vessels, all based on consuming cursed objects.

### Living Vessel

Living Vessels are a rarity, as resisting cursed objects is extremely difficult. Once a creature resists a cursed object, they become a vessel for it. This usually leads to a life of suffering due to sharing a body with someone who wants to overtake it.

* **Unauthorized Takeover.** Whenever you are knocked unconscious, you must make a Wisdom saving throw against the cursed object's DC. On a failed save, the cursed object takes control of your body for a number of rounds equal to 3 + the curse's CR. At the beginning of the cursed object's turn, you can repeat the saving throw, ending the possession on a success. If the cursed object takes control of your body while you have 0 hit points, it regains 1 hit point.
* **Innate Talk.** Whether you wish it or not, the cursed object within you can force you to communicate with it. At any point, the cursed object can draw you into its Innate Domain, a manifestation of its soul. While there, you can communicate with the cursed object until it releases you and returns you to the real world. Regardless of how long the conversation lasts within the Innate Domain, only 1 second passes in the real world.
* **Two Souls, One Body.** Your body houses another soul, forcing your own soul to adapt to its presence. This unusual coexistence makes you resistant to outside interference. You have resistance to psychic damage.

### Reincarnated

Reincarnated characters were fully reincarnated into their vessel's body, becoming the new owner of the body.

* **Life Experience.** This is not your first shot at life, and last time you learned a few things. You gain proficiency in two skills or one skill and one tool of your choice.
* **Cling on to Life.** You have died once and will make sure that it will not happen again. Whenever you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you may choose to be reduced to 1 hit point instead. You can do this once per long rest.
* **Full Suppression.** The other soul within the body you inhabit is so crushed and dominated that it is as if it was never there. You do not gain Soul Outline Awareness or the Soul Learning trait from being a Cursed Vessel.

### Symbiotic Vessel

The cursed object inside a symbiotic vessel does not take over the person. Instead, they form a symbiotic relationship with the vessel.

* **Always Awake.** Your cursed object is always watching for danger around you. Attacks do not gain advantage because they are unseen by you.
* **Experience of Two.** The cursed object inside you tells you about its past life, helping you with some things. Whenever you make a skill check, you may make it with advantage as long as your cursed object knows about the subject. You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses after a long rest.

> **Design Note:** The Incomplete Vessel and Complete Vessel subraces can only be used by someone who became a Cursed Object using the Cursed Object Creation feat.

### Incomplete Vessel

* **Owner's Will.** When you possessed the body, the host was not willing to give it to you and tried to stop you by limiting your power. Whenever you use features from your original body, such as your cursed technique, to harm creatures or things the body owner cared about, your power is greatly reduced. Your damage is reduced to one-tenth of its total whenever you deal damage, rounded up, and your range is reduced to one-tenth of its maximum, rounded to the nearest 5 feet, unless it has a touch range.
* **Destruction of Will.** You can remove the host's will to live completely and submerge the body in darkness. First, submerge yourself in a pool of blood for an entire short rest. Then kill a loved one of the creature using only its features, or destroy the thing it was most affectionate toward in life. After doing both things, the soul sinks into darkness and loses its will to live. You then become a Complete Vessel.

### Complete Vessel

* **Soul Damage.** You have learned to use the soul trapped inside you to negate damage. You have a pool of hit points equal to the maximum hit points the creature you possess used to have. Whenever you receive damage, you can spend cursed energy up to your Charisma modifier plus your proficiency bonus to remove 10 hit points from the pool per cursed energy spent. The damage is reduced by the amount spent. You regain all hit points in the pool after a long rest.
* **Perfect Reincarnation.** You have learned a method to reincarnate in your original body. After spending at least 1 day as a Complete Vessel, you can, as an action or reaction to taking damage, transform your body into its original form. You gain all benefits from your original body while losing the ability scores, size, speed, New Body Functions, Body Properties, and This Body Is Mine features gained from being a Cursed Vessel. The soul remains inside you. Additionally, you regain hit points up to your original body's hit point maximum. You can do this only if you are level 20 and have 40 or more Soul Knowledge.

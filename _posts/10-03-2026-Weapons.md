A taser is a less-lethal device primarily used by law enforcement that channels electricity into a target through a pair of barbs. If the target takes damage from a ranged attack with this weapon, they must attempt a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they are shocked and their speed is reduced to 0 until the end of their next turn. On a success, their movement speed is halved until the end of their next turn. If a creature fails by 5 or more, they are stunned until the end of their next turn instead. Creatures that are resistant to lightning damage have advantage on this saving throw, and creatures that are immune to lightning damage are immune to this effect.

**Ballistic Knife.** You can press a switch on the knife to cause it to shoot outward, making a special ranged attack with it at a range of 30 feet that deals 4d4 piercing damage on a hit instead of its normal damage. Hit or miss, once you make this attack the knife becomes unusable as a weapon until you spend an action to rewind it.

**Nunchaku.** When used by a wielder proficient with martial weapons or a monk, against a Small or Medium humanoid creature, the wielder gains a +2 bonus to attack rolls when attempting to disarm a creature (see Disarm, DMG 271).

**Tonfa Grip Style.** Using an object interaction, you can change the way you are gripping your tonfa to gain one of the following:

* **Standard Grip.** This weapon's damage die becomes 1d6.
* **Reverse Grip.** As a reaction to an attack roll, your AC increases by 1 against that attack.
* **Special Grip.** You gain a +2 bonus to Strength (Athletics) checks to grapple or shove a creature.

Rocket launchers are military devices primarily used to destroy buildings and vehicles in a single shot using a self-propelled explosive projectile. Each creature within 30 feet of where you fire this weapon must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw or take 10d6 fire damage. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage.

### Siege Weapon

A rocket launcher deals twice as much damage against objects and structures.

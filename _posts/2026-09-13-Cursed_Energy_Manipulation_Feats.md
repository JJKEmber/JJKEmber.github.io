These feats relate to how you manipulate Cursed Energy.

### Cursed Energy Manipulator

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 13 or higher; Cursed Energy Enhancement.

You have been trying out new ways to use your cursed energy and managed to learn a new method of using it.

Your Charisma score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
You may increase the tier of one of your Cursed Energy Enhancements. (From Novice to Advanced, or Advanced to Expert)
This feat may be taken multiple times.

### Advanced Energy Usage

**Prerequisite:** At least one level in the Jujutsu Sorcerer class.

You’ve learned more ways to use Cursed Energy in tandem with your other abilities, you will gain the following:

Your Charisma score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
You will gain two options from the Advanced Energy Usage class feature.
You can take this feat a total of two times.

### Cursed Enhanced Body

**Prerequisites:** Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution 16 or higher; Charisma 18 or higher; 8th level.

Your entire body is enhanced by cursed energy, making you excel in tasks people who train their bodies all their lives can't. You gain the following benefits:

Whenever you make a Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence or Wisdom saving throw, you may spend Cursed Energy up to your Charisma modifier as a reaction to add the number of energy spent as a bonus to the saving throw.
When making a melee weapon attack roll, you may add half of your Charisma modifier (rounded down) to your attack and damage rolls. This only applies if you currently have 1 cursed energy or more.

### Cursed Energy Slash

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 16 or higher; Cursed Weapon Enhancement at Advanced tier.

As an action while Cursed Weapon Enhancement is active on a weapon that deals slashing damage, you can spend Cursed Energy up to your Charisma modifier to produce a huge cursed energy slash. You make all creatures in a 30ft. line make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take the damage of your weapon + your Cursed Weapon Enhancement damage + a number of additional die equal to the amount of cursed energy spent in force damage, on a success they take half as much damage.

If the damage of your weapon has multiple dice, such as a Greatsword you instead add a number of additional d12s equal to the amount of cursed energy spent in force damage.

### Cursed Energy Ray

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 16 or higher.

You have learned how to create huge blasts out of pure cursed energy. As an action (S-2) and by spending cursed energy up to your Charisma modifier, you can launch a cursed energy ball in a line of 30ft. Once it reaches the end of the line, it will explode in a 15ft. circle, making every creature in that range make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take 1d12 + a number of additional dice equal to the amount spent in force damage, and half as much on a success.

### Cursed Energy Explosion

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 16 or higher.

You are capable of releasing a destructive burst of Cursed Energy from your body. As an Action by spending Cursed Energy up to your Charisma Modifier, you can release a burst of raw Cursed Energy. Every creature within a 30ft circle of the caster must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure they take 1d12 + a number of additional dice equal to the amount spent in force damage and are knocked back to the edges of the circle. They take half as much damage on a success and are not knocked back.

### Improved Cursed Energy Output

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 18 or higher; 12th level.

Through a long and difficult process, you have managed to strengthen your cursed energy output which can now be used to amplify your Jujutsu techniques further. As apart of any feature which uses cursed energy, you can expend extra cursed energy on it equal to half of the feature’s original cost rounded up to amplify its output. This cannot be used on Domain Expansion and Cursed Energy Enhancement.

**Amplification Benefits.** Amplifying a feature this way grants one of the following benefits:

The feature’s number of damage dice increases by half of your Charisma modifier, rounded down. If the feature does not deal damage dice, it’s damage bonus increases by your Charisma score.
The feature’s range increases by 5 times your Charisma modifier + 1 in feet. This range increase does not work with touch-based techniques.
The feature’s bonus to hit if it is a cursed energy attack roll, is increased by +2
The feature’s DC increases by +2
The feature’s duration increases by a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier + 1 so long as its duration is at least a minute by base.
**Cursed Enhancement.** You also have increased general Cursed Energy output and potency. Whenever you use a damage-dealing feature that utilizes Cursed Energy, its damage dice increase by one tier (d6 -> d8 -> d10). This does not apply to Cursed Energy Enhancements.

If a feature benefits from either Maximum Output or Chanting that is already benefiting from this feat, it’s total cursed energy cost is reduced by your Charisma modifier to a minimum of 1.

### Legendary Cursed Energy Output

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 20 or higher; all Cursed Energy Enhancements at Expert tier; Improved Cursed Energy Output; 15th level.

Your cursed energy output has become so efficient that the power you generate is legendary and your more generalized applications are something that only masters possess.

You will gain the following benefits:

Your Cursed Strike deals additional damage equal to your Charisma modifier + 1 (A Minimum of 1 to a maximum of +6). You can also expend additional cursed energy equal to half of your proficiency bonus, rounded down to double the damage bonus from this feat.
The amount of cursed energy you may spend on Cursed Armor is increased by your half of your proficiency bonus (rounded up).
Your Cursed Weapon Enhancement will deal additional damage equal to half of the cursed energy expended, rounded down + 1 (to a maximum of +6), so long as you are wielding the weapon which has the enhancement applied to it. When you activate Cursed Weapon Enhancement, you may expend additional cursed energy equal to half your proficiency bonus, rounded down, to double the damage bonus provided by this enhancement.
If you have the Cursed Energy Slash, you can decide if it is a 60ft line, 30ft cone or 20ft radius around you (you are excluded from making the saving throw) which deals double damage to structures or objects in range. If you have the Cursed Energy Ray feat, you can change the Dexterity saving throw into either a Strength or Constitution saving throw and it deals double damage to structures or objects in range. If you have the Cursed Energy Explosion feat, the range of the explosion increases by 10ft for every cursed energy expended and it now deals double damage to structures or objects in range.
Finally if you use the Improved Cursed Energy Output and use either Maximum Output or Chanting on the feature, it’s total cursed energy cost will now be reduced by your Charisma modifier + half of your proficiency bonus, rounded up, to a minimum of 1.
### Cursed Energy Output

### Advanced Efficiency

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 18 or higher; 2 Cursed Energy Enhancement options at Expert tier; 11th level.

You have increased your skill with cursed energy efficiency. You gain additional selections to reduce the cursed energy cost of as found in the Efficiency Fundamentals feature. The amount is equal to half of your Jujutsu Sorcerer level, rounded down. Whenever you reach an even level in the Jujutsu Sorcerer Class, you gain another selection.

Additionally, you gain a specialized selection. Choose one selection you possess. That feature's cost is instead reduced by your full proficiency bonus, to a minimum of 1 cursed energy.

### Flawless Fundamentals

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 20 or higher; Advanced Efficiency; 16th level.

You have become very skilled at efficiency with utilizing cursed energy, far beyond that of the average sorcerer. Your selections are now reduced by your full proficiency bonus rather than half. In addition, your specialized selection is now reduced by double your proficiency bonus.

### Unique Property Manipulation

**Prerequisites:** Unique Cursed Energy; 11th level.

You have learned to manipulate your Cursed Energy Property to create a special move related to manipulating its property. Below is an example of what a creature with lightning as their cursed energy property can develop, which you should use to create your special technique.

#### Example: Lightning Discharge

Whenever you deal damage to a creature with Cursed Strike or Cursed Weapon Enhancement, you may spend Cursed Energy up to your proficiency bonus to add one positive charge per energy spent. After a positive charge is applied, you may spend a bonus action to apply a negative charge. This creates a reaction, sending lightning to the target if it is within 300 feet and creating a line of lightning along its path. The target makes a Constitution saving throw at disadvantage. On a failure, it takes xd12 lightning damage, where x is the number of charges. On a success, it takes half damage. Creatures in the path make a Dexterity saving throw, taking the same damage on a failure and none on a success. You can apply positive charges to a target up to your proficiency bonus times your Charisma modifier. A creature with positive charges cannot dodge or avoid the discharge.

### High Output Mastery

**Prerequisites:** 13th level; Charisma 18 or higher.

Your ability to use large scale sorcery improves. You learn one of the options from the High Output Sorcery feature that you don’t already know.

You can take this feat a total of two times.

### Falling Blossom Emotion

**Prerequisite:** Sorcerer Family background.

Unlike Simple Domain, in which the user manifests their own domain to nullify a domain expansion's auto-hit attack, Falling Blossom Emotion shrouds the user in cursed energy that counter-attacks the moment a domain's guaranteed hit makes contact. As an action or reaction (S-2) against being affected by a sure hit effect of a Domain Expansion, or seeing a Domain Expansion open, for 10 Cursed Energy, you will shroud yourself in cursed energy for 1 minute. The sure-hit feature of the Domain Expansion targetting or affecting you is negated. This prevents any Cursed Energy attack rolls from automatically hitting you and prevents you from automatically failing any Cursed Energy saves caused by the Domain Expansion. You gain a special damage reduction equal to your Charisma modifier times your proficiency bonus added twice plus your Charisma modifier times 4 that applies to the sure hit effect for the duration of this technique. If the damage from the Domain Expansion does not exceed the damage reduction, its damage will be negated and any effects it would apply are negated as well. This feature cannot prevent the effects or damage of anything non-physical, such as a mental attack which does psychic damage.

You may also use this technique offensively. As an action for 5 Cursed Energy, you can surround yourself with cursed energy and prepare to make counterattacks for the next minute. When you receive a melee attack roll, you can use your reaction to make one melee weapon attack with advantage against the attacking creature. On a hit, the attack is considered as a critical hit and will deal its damage as force damage. This attack only counts as a critical hit for the sake of damage and not things like determining Black Flashes. While maintaining concentration on Falling Blossom Emotion, you may continue to counterattack against melee attack rolls as long as you have the reactions.

If your cursed energy alters damage types for features, it can also alter the damage type for Falling Blossom Emotion.

You must maintain concentration on Falling Blossom Emotion to keep it active.

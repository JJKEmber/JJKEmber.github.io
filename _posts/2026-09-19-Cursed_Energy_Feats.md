These feats relate to Cursed Energy in its pure and fundamental form.

### Immense Cursed Energy

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 15 or higher.

Your Cursed Energy has massive reserves, making you a Cursed Energy powerhouse.

* Your Charisma score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* Your Cursed Energy maximum increases by your proficiency bonus and your Charisma modifier.

### Overflowing Cursed Energy

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 17 or higher; Immense Cursed Energy.

Your Cursed Energy reserves are practically endless, allowing you to perform great feats of jujutsu when others run out of Cursed Energy.

* Your Charisma score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* Add half your character level, rounded up, plus your proficiency bonus when calculating your Cursed Energy maximum.

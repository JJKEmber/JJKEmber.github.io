# Cursed Corpse

Cursed corpses are artificial beings created through cursed energy. Some possess the souls of deceased sorcerers, while others are constructed with multiple cores and specialized modifications.

## Cursed Corpse Traits

### Ability Score Increase

Increase one ability score of your choice by 2 and a different ability score by 1. Alternatively, increase three different ability scores of your choice by 1. You can't increase an ability score above 20 using this trait.

### Age

You can live for a very long time, as you do not age.

### Alignment

Cursed corpses can be any alignment, but they generally share one with the soul they are made from.

### Size

You are Medium or Small. You choose your size when you select this race.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Constructed Being

You don't need to eat, drink, or breathe, and you don't need to sleep. You are immune to the poisoned condition and poison damage.

Your creature type is Construct, specifically a Cursed Corpse.

Because you are not naturally born, you cannot gain a Cursed Technique unless you are a Reincarnated Corpse.

As long as your cursed corpse core is intact, you can be revived. You cannot regain hit points through Reverse Cursed Technique.

### Cursed Corpse Mobility

Your body can be constructed with specialized movement mechanisms. Choose one of the following options when you select this race.

#### Water Propeller

Your body is equipped with one or more propellers that allow you to travel through water.

You gain a swimming speed of 20 feet.

#### Treads

Your body is equipped with tank-like treads designed to navigate difficult terrain.

You gain a ground speed of 25 feet. When moving through difficult terrain, your speed cannot be reduced below 25 feet as a result of the terrain. After determining your speed from difficult terrain, apply any other effects that would modify your speed normally.

#### Wheels

Your body is equipped with wheels designed for rapid movement across smooth surfaces.

You gain a rolling speed of 40 feet. When moving through difficult terrain, your rolling speed is reduced to 20 feet.

#### Thrusters

Your body is equipped with miniature jet engines capable of propelling you into the air.

You gain a flying speed of 30 feet and can hover.

If you are incapacitated while flying, your flying speed becomes 0 and you fall unless you have another means of remaining aloft.

### Languages

You can speak, read, and write Common and one other language of your choice.

## Cursed Corpse Subraces

### Reincarnated Corpse

A Reincarnated Corpse contains the soul of a deceased sorcerer.

#### Cursed Technique

Because your soul once had a Cursed Technique, you can gain one as a corpse.

#### Cling to Life

You have died once, and your soul does not want that to happen again. Whenever you are reduced to 0 hit points, you can choose to be reduced to 1 hit point instead.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

#### Cunning Experience

Having already lived and died once, you possess a wealth of experience and a cunning born from a previous life. When you fail an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw, you can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to the result.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

#### Deathless Experience

The knowledge and instincts of your former life remain within you. You gain proficiency in one skill and one tool of your choice.

#### Soul's Persistence

Your soul refuses to be separated from your cursed corpse. You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, frightened, or possessed.

### Abrupt-Mutation Cursed Corpse

An Abrupt-Mutation Cursed Corpse is powered by three separate cores. Each core contains an animal-based modification.

#### Multiple Cores

Your body has three cores that power it. When you select this subrace, choose three different cores from the options below.

As a bonus action, you can switch between your cores, activating the animal modification contained within the chosen core.

#### Core Options

##### Panda Core

You can add your Charisma modifier to the damage of your unarmed attacks.

##### Triceratops Core

You gain a Charge attack. As an action, you can charge toward a creature within your speed range and make an attack roll against it. You must move at least 10 feet in a straight line toward the target before making the attack.

On a hit, the target takes three rolls of your unarmed damage as piercing damage and must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. On a successful save, the target is unaffected beyond the damage.

##### T-Rex Core

You gain a Bite attack. When you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with a bite.

On a hit, the bite deals twice your unarmed damage dice as piercing damage.

##### Reindeer Core

You gain a Charge feature. When you move at least 10 feet straight toward a creature and hit it with an attack on the same turn, the attack deals one additional die of your unarmed damage.

##### Diabolical Ironclad Beetle Core

You gain damage reduction equal to twice your Constitution modifier.

##### Tiger Core

You gain the ability to Sneak Attack. Once per turn, when you have advantage on an attack roll against a creature, you can deal an additional 2d6 damage to the target on a hit.

##### Lion Core

As a bonus action, you can roar and rally your allies. Up to three creatures of your choice within 30 feet of you gain a +2 bonus to their next attack roll or ability check.

This benefit lasts for 1 minute or until used.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

##### Honey Badger Core

You gain a burrowing speed equal to half your walking speed.

##### African Rain Frog Core

You can gulp down a creature. If a Medium or smaller creature is grappled by you, you can use an action to swallow it.

Until the grapple ends, the swallowed creature is restrained and has total cover from creatures outside of you.

You can have no more than one creature swallowed at a time.

##### Cheetah Core

**Feline Agility.** When you move on your turn in combat, you can double your speed until the end of the turn.

Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you move 0 feet on one of your turns.

##### Platypus Core

You have venomous spurs. When a creature makes a melee attack against you, you can use your reaction to strike the attacker with your spurs. The attacker must make a Constitution saving throw.

On a failed save, the creature is poisoned for 1 minute. On a successful save, it isn't poisoned.

The poisoned creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on itself on a success.

##### Pangolin Core

You gain resistance to nonmagical slashing and piercing damage.

##### Ram Core

You gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed.

##### Elephant Core

You count as two sizes larger when determining your carrying capacity and the creatures you can grapple.

##### Orca Core

You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed and can breathe underwater.

While underwater, you have 60 feet of blindsight.

##### Lynx Core

You gain Keen Senses. You have 60 feet of darkvision, and you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight or hearing.

##### Parrot Core

You can mimic any sound or phrase you have heard within the past hour.

You have advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks made while mimicking a sound.

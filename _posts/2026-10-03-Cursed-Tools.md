Cursed tools are weapons and support items used by jujutsu sorcerers or Heavenly Restriction users that are purposefully imbued with a curse. They can be found scattered across the world, but most are owned by Jujutsu High or are heavily guarded in clan warehouses such as those of the Zenin or Kamo families. Cursed tools count as magic items and objects.

## Cursed Damage

Almost all cursed tools have cursed energy imbued into them, making them stronger. They add an additional die equal to the weapon's base damage die in force damage. For every grade above four, this additional damage die increases by one tier.

For example:

- Grade 4 Katana: 1d8 slashing damage + 1d8 force damage
- Grade 3 Katana: 1d8 slashing damage + 1d10 force damage
- Grade 2 Katana: 1d8 slashing damage + 1d12 force damage
- Grade 1 Katana: 1d8 slashing damage + 2d6 force damage
- Special Grade Katana: 1d8 slashing damage + 2d8 force damage

Additionally, some cursed tools gain a bonus to attack and damage rolls based on their grade. Generally, a Grade 2 cursed tool has a +1 bonus, a Grade 1 cursed tool has a +2 bonus, and a Special Grade cursed tool has a +3 bonus.

## Cursed Tool Durability

In the world of jujutsu, weapons and cursed tools have durability that reflects their resilience against physical impacts and supernatural force. The durability of an item decreases with usage, especially in high-energy battles, and can be damaged or destroyed in intense encounters.

| Grade | Durability | Damage Reduction |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Grade 4 | 50 | 50 |
| Grade 3 | 100 | 100 |
| Grade 2 | 150 | 150 |
| Grade 1 | 200 | 200 |
| Special Grade | 500 | 400 |

### Conditions for Reducing Durability

- **Area Effects.** If the creature holding the cursed tool is subjected to an area of effect save, the cursed tool takes the same damage as the wielder would, unless stated otherwise.
- **Attacks Directly Targeting the Item.** If a creature specifically targets a cursed tool, they must succeed on an attack roll against its AC, determined by grade plus the wielder's Dexterity or Strength modifier, chosen by the wielder.
- **Extreme Temperature or Corrosive Elements.** Items exposed to these environments take 1d6 damage every minute, which ignores damage reduction.

| Grade | Base Armor Class |
| --- | ---: |
| Grade 4 | 10 |
| Grade 3 | 13 |
| Grade 2 | 15 |
| Grade 1 | 20 |
| Special Grade | 30 |

### Durability Loss Effects

As durability decreases, items lose effectiveness. Each threshold below imposes unique drawbacks.

| Durability | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 50% | -1 to hit, and the damage die tier is reduced by 1. If the die tier cannot be reduced, it instead takes a -10 damage penalty. |
| 25% | -2 to hit, and the damage die tier is reduced by 2. If the die tier cannot be reduced, it instead takes a -20 damage penalty. |
| 0% | The weapon shatters and becomes unusable. |

### Repairing Durability

**Basic Repairs.** A creature possessing the Hiroshima Advanced Studies feature, or the Cursed Blacksmith feat, can restore full durability during an 8-hour period, requiring cursed energy or high-quality materials worth at least a tenth of the item's value.

## Semi-Cursed Tools

A semi-cursed tool is created when a sorcerer constantly imbues cursed energy into their weapon, making it filled with cursed energy. Additionally, one extra semi-cursed tool has been added below.

### Cursed Gloves
*Wondrous item, Common*

**Gloves Imbued with Cursed Energy.** While wearing these gloves, the wearer's unarmed strikes count as magical for the purposes of overcoming resistances and immunities. They can be created like any other semi-cursed tool, but they must be created by infusing gloves rather than a weapon.

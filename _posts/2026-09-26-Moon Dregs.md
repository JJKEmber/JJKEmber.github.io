Moon Dregs
Tiny monstrosity (shikigami), unaligned

Armor Class 16 (natural armor)
Hit Points 11 (2d4(It gains another two dice every time the summoner levels up))
Speed 0 ft., 30ft. fly.

STR	DEX	CON	INT	WIS	CHA
16 (+3)	12 (+1)	17 (+3)	6 (-2)	11 (+0)	12 (+1)
Proficiency Bonus +2
Damage Resistances Bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage from non-magical sources.
Damage Immunities Poison
Condition Immunities poisoned
Senses passive Perception 10
Languages Understands it's masters' languages but can't speak them.
Challenge 1 (200 XP)

Invisible Force. Moon Dregs is entirely made out of cursed energy, meaning it cannot be seen by normal people. Creatures without levels in the Jujutsu Sorcerer class or who cannot see into the ethereal plane cannot see it. Although, if these creatures were in a life and death situation(less than half their maximum hit points), they gain the ability to see it.

Cursed Weapons. The Moon Dregs' weapon attacks are magical.

Debilitating Poison. Whenever a creature fails Moon Dreg's poison saving throw, it will gain the following effects depending on the severity of the failure:

The target loses 10 ft. of its movement speed if it fails by 1 or more, to a minimum of 10 feet.
The target is knocked prone if it fails by 2 or more.
The target is affected by these effects for 1 minute and its effects are cumulative. A creature is able to repeat the Constitution saving throw at the beginning of its turns, ending the effects early on a success. Succeeding on the saving throw to remove the additional effects removes the poisoned condition, but does not grant immunity against getting afflicted with them again.

Body Protection. Moon Dregs can use an action or reaction to swallow a willing creature within its reach inside its body, allowing the creature to remain inside of it. Creatures inside Moon Dregs are under the effects of full cover, but cannot take actions until they leave by spending half of their movement. Every time Moon Dregs moves, the creature moves along as well. Moon Dregs cannot use Body Protection on creatures larger than itself, or if it has a creature swallowed already.

ACTIONS
Multiattack. The Moon Dregs makes two of the same attack, or one attack with its tentacles and another with its Stinger.

Tentacles Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage. On a hit, the target must make a Constitution saving throw against the summoner's cursed energy DC or become poisoned until the end of their next turn.

Stinger. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage + 7 (1d8 + 3) poison damage. On a hit, the target must make a Constitution saving throw against the summoner's cursed energy DC or become poisoned for one minute. The affected creature can attempt this saving throw again at the beginning of their next turn.

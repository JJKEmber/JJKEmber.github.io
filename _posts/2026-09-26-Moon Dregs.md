## Traits

### Invisible Force

Moon Dregs is entirely made of cursed energy and cannot be seen by ordinary people. Creatures without levels in the Jujutsu Sorcerer class or the ability to see into the Ethereal Plane cannot see it. If such a creature is in a life-or-death situation (at less than half its maximum hit points), it gains the ability to see Moon Dregs.

### Cursed Weapons

Moon Dregs' weapon attacks are magical.

### Debilitating Poison

Whenever a creature fails Moon Dregs' poison saving throw, it suffers the following effects based on how much it failed by:

- **Failure by 1 or more:** The target's speed is reduced by 10 feet, to a minimum of 10 feet.
- **Failure by 2 or more:** The target is knocked prone.

These effects last for 1 minute and are cumulative. The creature can repeat the Constitution saving throw at the beginning of each of its turns, ending the effects early on a success. A successful save to end the additional effects also ends the poisoned condition, but does not grant immunity to being afflicted again.

### Body Protection

Moon Dregs can use an action or reaction to swallow a willing creature within its reach. A creature inside Moon Dregs has total cover but cannot take actions. It can leave by spending half its movement. Whenever Moon Dregs moves, the creature moves with it. Moon Dregs cannot use Body Protection on a creature larger than itself or while it already has a creature swallowed.

## Actions

### Multiattack

Moon Dregs makes two attacks of the same kind, or one Tentacles attack and one Stinger attack.

### Tentacles

*Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage. On a hit, the target must make a Constitution saving throw against the summoner's cursed energy DC or become poisoned until the end of its next turn.

### Stinger

*Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage plus 7 (1d8 + 3) poison damage. On a hit, the target must make a Constitution saving throw against the summoner's cursed energy DC or become poisoned for 1 minute. The target can attempt this saving throw again at the beginning of its next turn.

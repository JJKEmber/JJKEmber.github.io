Weapon feats are dedicated to the art of mastering weapons.

### Battle Veteran

**Prerequisites:** Wisdom 12 or higher; 10th level.

You have experienced many battles and are exceptional at learning from them.

* You gain 2 Secret Techniques. At 17th level, you gain another Secret Technique.
* You can forgo taking a Secret Technique to gain the Power Style feature from Bukijutsu Sorcery instead.
* If you possess the Weapon Master Sorcerer Path, you instead gain 4 Secret Techniques. At 13th and 17th level, you gain another Secret Technique.

### Cursed Weapon Wielder

**Prerequisites:** Strength or Dexterity 18 or higher; 8th level.

Your mastery of weaponry has made you an ideal wielder of powerful cursed tools.

* Weapon attacks using a Cursed Tool or a weapon affected by Cursed Weapon Enhancement have their critical hit range increased by 1. For example, a weapon that normally scores a critical hit on a roll of 20 can score one on a roll of 19 or 20.
* When you roll a natural 20 on an attack roll for a Cursed Tool or a weapon affected by Cursed Weapon Enhancement, you can make one additional weapon attack with advantage using the same weapon. This additional attack cannot trigger another additional attack.
* You may ignore one property of a weapon you are wielding.

### Cursed Tool Energy Manipulation

**Prerequisites:** Cursed Weapon Wielder; Cursed Weapon Enhancement at Expert tier.

You have learned to use the cursed energy of a cursed tool to fuel your jujutsu. While holding a cursed tool, you can use a bonus action to extract an amount of cursed energy up to your cursed energy modifier plus your proficiency bonus. You gain that amount as temporary cursed energy until the end of the turn. The energy in the tool resets after a long rest.

| Cursed Tool Grade | Cursed Energy Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Grade 4 | 5 |
| Grade 3 | 10 |
| Grade 2 | 15 |
| Grade 1 | 20 |
| Special Grade | 25 |

You may use energy only from actual cursed tools, not tools temporarily summoned by a cursed technique, such as G Warstaff.

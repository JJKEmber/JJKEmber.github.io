Barrier techniques are cursed techniques used to create barriers. Most sorcerers can grasp the basics of barrier techniques, but only a few can truly master them.

### Basic Barrier

**Prerequisite:** Charisma 14 or higher.

You have learned how to create basic barriers to protect areas or trap opponents. You can spend up to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1) in cursed energy as an action (S-1), creating a dome starting from yourself with a 5ft. radius and a 10ft. height. For each cursed energy spent, the barrier’s maximum radius and height both increase by 10ft, but you may choose to make it smaller if you wish. This barrier has 10 hit points times the amount of cursed energy spent, it has resistance to all damage from the inside, is vulnerable to all damage from the outside and is immune to Poison and Psychic damage. This barrier has an AC equal to the caster's Cursed Energy DC and automatically fails saving throws. This barrier lasts a number of minutes equal to your Charisma modifier. You can deactivate your basic barrier at will (no action required).

#### Curtain

*Emerge from the darkness, blacker than darkness. Purify that which is impure.*

As an action (S-1, V-1) for 5 Cursed Energy, you cover the sky and the ground within a 2165-foot radius with a sphere of darkness. Upon activation, a dark, almost liquid-like matter drips down from the sky to form the outer barrier wall in the shape of a sphere that extends underground, appearing as a dome from the surface. The curtain changes the sky becomes dark despite the time of day, provoking any cursed spirits out of hiding. Those without levels in the Jujutsu Sorcerer class cannot see anything inside the barrier, merely seeing as if nothing but the vegetation was there. Creatures who can see into the ethereal plane or have levels in the jujutsu sorcerer path can see the black sphere.

The curtain lasts for 10 times your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1) minutes. Your curtain has a number of hit points equal to your Charisma modifier times half your Jujutsu Sorcerer level (rounded down). This barrier has an AC equal to the caster's Cursed Energy DC and automatically fails saving throws. It is also immune to Poison and Psychic damage.

You may choose to make your curtain smaller in radius, however it cannot be smaller than a 100-foot radius.

**Conditions.** Your Curtain can have a condition for what can and cannot pass through the barrier, rather than barring entry to anything that possesses cursed energy. You can set the Curtain to restrict creatures with low cursed energy, such as creatures without levels in the Jujutsu Sorcerer class, while allowing creatures with high cursed energy to pass. These conditions must be simple and basic.

### Barrier Expert

**Prerequisites:** Basic Barrier; Charisma 18 or higher.

You have studied barriers and their effects for a long time, making you become an extremely vital resource for the Jujutsu world.

Whenever you summon your curtain, its hit points will now be your Charisma modifier times your Jujutsu Sorcerer level.
Your basic barrier's hit points will now be 20 times the amount of cursed energy spent.
The Conditions you place on your curtain can have somewhat expanded complexity, like potentially barring entrance to all things without cursed energy, while allowing anything with cursed energy to pass through as a tradeoff.

### Barrier Invader

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 18 or higher; Basic Barrier.

You have learned how to invade barriers without breaking them. You will gain the following benefits:

Once per round, while outside a barrier, you can make a Intelligence (Arcana) check that adds your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1) against the cursed save dc of the sorcerer who created the barrier to invade it. On a failure, you won't be allowed to enter. On a success, you will enter the barrier although the creator will know a creature has entered. If you had Cursed Energy Concealer active, the creator does not know a creature has entered their barrier.
Once per round, while inside a barrier, you may make a Intelligence (Arcana) check that adds your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1) against the cursed save dc of the sorcerer to alter its properties. On a failure, you cannot mess with the barrier. On a success, you may change the barrier's size, length, creatures allowed to enter or leave or any other properties you can modify.
None of the features above affect domain expansions.

### New Shadow Style: Simple Domain

The main technique of the new shadow style, also known as "the domain for the weak."

**Vow.** To use Simple Domain properly, a Jujutsu Sorcerer must use the Simple Domain Binding Vow.

As an action or reaction (S-2) against a cursed energy attack roll or saving throw, or domain expansion opening, or being affected by the sure hit effect of a Domain Expansion, for 5 Cursed Energy, you surround yourself with a 5 ft. barrier for 1 minute, though said barrier does not impede movement. The circle has an amount of hit points equal to half of your proficiency bonus (rounded down) times your refinement points. While inside of a Domain Expansion, the sure-hit feature and sure-hit effect is negated against creatures inside the simple domain but at the start of your turn the simple domain will take 3d12 force damage per 100 refinement the domain has. If the simple domain is reduced to 0 hit points, this technique ends.

**Output Dampening.** Simple Domain cannot neutralize innate techniques themselves, but it protects by lowering their output. Any innate technique that targets you, creatures inside your Simple Domain, or an area overlapping your Simple Domain has its damage dice reduced by your proficiency bonus, to a minimum of 0. If it does not deal damage, its Cursed Save DC is reduced by your proficiency bonus. While in your Simple Domain, creatures other than you cannot spend more Cursed Energy than 50 minus your Jujutsu Sorcerer level, before reductions, in a single expenditure. This does not stop the casting of Domain Expansion. When using Cursed Concentration, only damage reduced by this feature is affected.

**Output Increase.** Any cursed energy feature you use while in your own Simple Domain has its damage dice tier increased by one. If it does not roll dice, its damage increases by your proficiency bonus.

**Reinforcement.** As an action while your Simple Domain is active, you can reduce your speed to 0 until the beginning of your next turn and cause your Simple Domain to regain hit points equal to your refinement points.

Your simple domain can be refined with time and practice, the more refined your simple domain is, the stronger it gets. You will start out with 1 refinement point. You have the following methods to refine your simple domain:

Training. On a long rest, you can spend a number of cursed energy up to your proficiency bonus, gaining an equal number of refinement points. You cannot regain this cursed energy until the next long rest. Once you reach 50 or more refinement, you can no longer gain refinement using this method.

Usage. The most effective way to refine your simple domain is by putting it to the test against the barrier of a Domain. Whenever your simple domain’s hit points are reduced by a Domain Expansion, you will gain 1 refinement point for every 10 damage it takes.

You gain benefits the more refined your simple domain is. The refinement benefits for each refinement level are described below:

| Benefit | Description | Refinement Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Barrier Durability** | Your Simple Domain's hit points equal your refinement points times half your proficiency bonus, rounded down. | 1+ |
| **Expansion** | You can cast your Simple Domain with a 10-foot or 15-foot radius. For every 5 feet beyond 5 feet, it loses 50 hit points. | 25+ |
| **Quicker Domain** | You can use your Simple Domain as a bonus action. | 70+ |
Your refinement points cannot go above 100.

To use this technique, you must maintain concentration as if you were concentrating on a spell. You cannot react to the effects of a Domain Expansion with this feat the first time you would get the opportunity after your simple domain ends or is broken.

### Simple Domain: Maculation

**Prerequisites:** 10th level; New Shadow Style Master.

You learn an advanced Simple Domain technique that allows you to increase its area without compromising its integrity by creating many smaller domains in a grid pattern. When you cast simple domain, you may spend 1 cursed energy to increase its radius by 5 feet to a maximum of 25 feet. It does not lose any hit points when you do so. If a creature is already within the radius of your Simple Domain when it's cast this way, it moving again while within it will count as it having entered it.

### Vowless Simple Domain

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 20 or higher; New Shadow Style: Simple Domain; 50 Simple Domain refinement.

You have gained enough skill with the Simple Domain technique that it no longer requires a binding vow to be made for it to be used. You no longer need a Simple Domain Binding Vow.

### Simple Domain Imbuement

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 20 or higher; Basic Barrier.

You have learned how to imbue a Simple Domain of the New Shadow Style into an item or object. At the end of a long rest, you may make a number of imbuements equal to your proficiency bonus, spending 2 cursed energy for each imbuement. You can choose to imbue your simple domain into a melee weapon, a piece of ammunition, or an object, which lasts until a long rest. When used on a melee weapon or piece of ammunition, when you hit an attack with them while it possesses an imbuement on it, you may expend an imbuement to allow that attack to bypass innate technique defenses. A weapon or piece of ammunition benefitting from Simple Domain Imbuement cannot have an innate technique feature used or active on it. In addition, damage from an attack affected by Simple Domain Imbuement counts as Minor Soul Harming.

In addition, while you have an imbued item in your possession, you may expend an imbuement to cast New Shadow Style: Simple Domain. Even if you do not possess the New Shadow Style: Simple Domain, you can use the normal methods within the feat to refine it, to a maximum refinement of 70.

### Refined Sorcerer

**Prerequisites:** Charisma 18 or higher; Domain Expansion or New Shadow Style: Simple Domain.

Domains are a trait that few sorcerers come to understand, much less master as the process is time-consuming and some sorcerers don't ever see their true potential… But a very selective few sorcerers have an uncanny understanding of domains. If you have a Simple Domain, all methods of refining the domain will provide additional refinement points equal to half of your proficiency bonus(rounded down) + 1. If you have a Domain Expansion, all methods of refining the domain will provide additional refinement points equal to your Charisma modifier added three times.

This does not apply to the Domain Training refinement method. Instead, this method will provide you with additional refinement points equal to your Charisma modifier + 1. In addition, the limit of domain training for you is changed. You are no longer limited to train your Domain at 500 refinement, the limit will instead become your proficiency bonus +1, then multiplied by 100. If this causes your threshold to become lower than 500, it will remain 500.

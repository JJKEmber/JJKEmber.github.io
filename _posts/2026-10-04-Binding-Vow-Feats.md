These feats relate to Binding Vows.

### Vows Master

**Prerequisites:** Intelligence 13 or higher; Binding Vow feature; 5th level.

You have become experienced in making vows with other people and yourself, leading you to make them more frequently.

- Your Intelligence score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Your binding vow limit increases to 2 + twice your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 4).

# Simurian

## Physical Description

Simurians look a lot like humans, with their most defining characteristic being their third eye in their forehead. Simurians might also have traits that humans do not, such as a cylindrical head, long earlobes, or horns as seen on the Deskunte people.

## History

On a far-off planet called Simuria exists a species of human-like beings called Simurians. All of them are born with a third eye that only sheds tears at their birth or death. Among the Rumelian tribe, only males possess rolloluca, known as cursed energy on Earth, which is stored in that eye. Due to tensions between Rumelian and Deskunte Simurians over the planet's natural resources and beasts, the Rumelians, along with Dabura Karaba, left Simuria in search of a new home and eventually arrived on Earth in the year 2086.

## Simurian Names

Simurian names are given to them at birth. For Rumelians specifically, their middle names are given by the village Kalyan.

### Third Eye

You have an additional eye on your forehead, granting you advantage on sight-based Perception checks. It is also the source of your rolloluca, or cursed energy, so you cannot use rolloluca if your eye is destroyed.

Once per long rest, if you fall unconscious during combat, your eye remains open, allowing you to continue fighting. You are berserk while unconscious in this manner. You stop fighting this way if you run out of cursed energy or stamina.

## Simurian Tribes

### Rumelian

Rumelians worship Kalyans, with both their culture and religion being deeply involved with the creatures.

* **Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity or Wisdom score increases by 1.
* **Kalyan Worship.** Due to your people worshipping Kalyans as gods, they do not attack you unprovoked. Cursed Spirits also remain passive around you, reducing the DC for domesticating cursed spirits by your proficiency bonus. Additionally, you gain proficiency in Religion.
#### Kaylan Familiar

Your people share a spiritual bond with Kaylans, the physical manifestations of cursed spirits.

As an action, you can call out to a Kaylan, causing it to reveal itself in an unoccupied space within 10 feet of you. When you call the Kaylan, choose its form: bat, cat, crab, frog (toad), hawk, lizard, octopus, poisonous snake, fish (quipper), rat, raven, seahorse, spider, or weasel.

The Kaylan uses the statistics of the chosen form, except its creature type is Cursed Spirit.

The Kaylan is naturally invisible. When it reveals itself in response to your call, it becomes visible. As a bonus action, the Kaylan can become invisible or visible. If the Kaylan makes an attack while invisible, it immediately becomes visible after making the attack.

Once you call forth a Kaylan, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

The Kaylan acts independently of you, but it always obeys your commands. In combat, the Kaylan rolls its own initiative and takes its own turn. A Kaylan can attack and take other actions as normal.

#### Soul Resonance

Your third eye allows you to communicate with your Kaylan through the resonance between your souls. While your Kaylan is within 100 feet of you, you can communicate with it telepathically. This connection doesn't require you or the Kaylan to share a language, as your souls resonate through their shared soul color.

Additionally, as an action, you can see through your Kaylan's eyes and hear what it hears until the start of your next turn. While doing so, you gain the benefits of any special senses the Kaylan possesses. During this time, you are blinded and deafened with regard to your own senses.

#### Kaylan's Passing

Your bond with your Kaylan is deeply spiritual. Because of your people's worship of Kaylans and the shared color of your souls, your third eye sheds tears when your Kaylan dies.

When your Kaylan dies, you must make a DC 25 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, you take 3d12 psychic damage. This damage can't be reduced or prevented in any way and is dealt directly to your hit points. On a successful save, you take half as much damage.

The Kaylan's physical body remains after its death.

### Deskunte

Deskunte is a nation of warriors where the strongest is the one who decides.

* **Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength or Constitution score increases by 1.
* **Darkvision.** Accustomed to hunting and fighting in darkness, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 120 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
* **Relentless Endurance.** When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 hit point instead. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
* **Athletic.** You have proficiency in the Athletics skill.
* **Cursed Horns.** The Deskunte's inherited killing intent causes cursed energy to gather and manifest as physical horns. You gain a pair of horns on your head, whose size depends on your maximum cursed energy. Your horns are natural melee weapons that you can use to make unarmed strikes. On a hit, they deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike. This damage die increases by one tier for every 15 maximum cursed energy or 7 Stamina you possess, to a maximum of three tier increases.

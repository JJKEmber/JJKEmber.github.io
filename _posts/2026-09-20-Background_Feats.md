### Otherworldly Body

**Prerequisite:** You are not a Cursed Corpse.

You were born with an extraordinary physique that surpasses the limits of an ordinary body.

Choose Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution. The maximum for the chosen ability score increases by 2. Additionally, the chosen ability score increases by 1 each time you gain a level, up to its new maximum. You also gain proficiency in saving throws using the chosen ability score.

Additionally, one of your other two physical ability scores of your choice increases by 1, up to its normal maximum.

The benefits you gain depend on the ability score you chose:

Strength. You gain the Athlete History feat. In addition, you add your Strength modifier to the damage of your melee attacks an additional time. You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and when resolving grapples, shoves, and disarms.

Dexterity. You gain the 50 Meters in 3 Seconds! feat. In addition, your walking speed increases by a number of feet equal to 5 times your Dexterity modifier, to a minimum increase of 5 feet.

**Constitution.** You gain the Strong Body feat. In addition, your hit point maximum increases by 3 whenever you gain a level in a class.

### Humanoid Prison

**Prerequisites:** Must be a human; must not be a Heavenly Restriction.

> **Design Note:** Despite this feat having one of its requirements the need to "be Human," if you become a Living Vessel for the sake of this feat you still count as human.

Your constitution is something to marvel at as you may consume Cursed Objects and suffer next to no afflictions from this act, a trait so rare that it appears in a human once every 1000 years. You have advantage on all checks and saving throws to resist any kind of Cursed Objects you consume, with their DC being reduced by your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier.

If you fail a saving throw when consuming a Cursed Object, you instead spit out the object within 5 feet of you as opposed to the normal effects the Cursed Object inflicts on a failed save. If the effect would kill you, you not only spit it out but are also knocked unconscious.

Lastly, you also gain the Strong Body feat.

A Cursed Object you consume cannot force you to submit your body over to them unless a Binding Vow has been made between you and the Cursed Object. If you consume more Cursed Objects than your level + your Constitution modifier, you must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, you are possessed for 1 minute.

As an action, you may "switch" with a Cursed Object you have consumed, giving them temporary control over your body in which you cannot swap back for a number of rounds equal to the cursed objects Charisma modifier. After those set amount of rounds you may as a reaction, make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw. On a success, you take control back from the Cursed Object whereas on a failure, you cannot take back control for 1 minute. The DC for this save increases by 1 for every success and is reduced by 1 for every failure. The DC returns to its original difficulty when you take a Long Rest.

### Unique Cursed Energy

**Prerequisite:** You do not have a Heavenly Restriction.

Your cursed energy possesses a rare and distinctive quality, granting it properties beyond those of ordinary cursed energy.

Choose one damage type, excluding force damage. This damage type becomes your Cursed Energy Trait.

Whenever you activate Cursed Armor, you gain resistance to your Cursed Energy Trait for the duration.

While your Cursed Armor is active, whenever a creature hits you with a melee attack, that creature takes damage of your Cursed Energy Trait equal to your level.

Whenever you deal damage with a Cursed Energy feature that isn't part of a Cursed Technique, the damage type of that damage becomes your Cursed Energy Trait.

At 14th level, whenever you use a feature whose damage type is changed to your Cursed Energy Trait, you can ignore resistance to that damage type. This feature does not allow you to ignore immunity.

Your Cursed Energy Trait also grants you one of the following benefits:

#### Wellspring of Energy
Your maximum Cursed Energy increases by an amount equal to your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier.

If you possess a Cursed Feat that increases your maximum Cursed Energy, your maximum Cursed Energy increases by an additional amount equal to your Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution modifier (your choice).

#### Dynamic Striker
Choose one condition that is appropriate to your Cursed Energy Trait. The chosen condition cannot be Incapacitated, nor can it be a condition that directly causes the Incapacitated condition.

Whenever you deal damage to a creature with a Cursed Energy feature that isn't an Innate Technique, you can force that creature to make a Constitution saving throw against your Cursed Energy save DC.

On a successful save, the creature is unaffected. On a failed save, the creature suffers your chosen condition until the end of its next turn.

This effect can trigger once for each attack or saving throw that deals damage to a creature. If an attack or saving throw causes a creature to take damage multiple times as part of the same attack or feature, it can trigger this effect only once for that creature.

If the creature uses Cursed Armor to reduce or mitigate the damage from the triggering feature, and the amount of Cursed Energy it spends on Cursed Armor is greater than the amount of Cursed Energy you spent on the triggering feature, the Dynamic Striker effect is negated.

#### Natural Resistance
You gain resistance to your Cursed Energy Trait. If you have Cursed Armor active, your resistance instead becomes immunity to your Cursed Energy Trait.

You cannot choose this option if your Cursed Energy Trait is bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage.

#### Weakness
The exceptional potency of your Cursed Energy Trait comes at a cost. Choose another damage type. You become vulnerable to that damage type.

If you choose to gain this vulnerability, you gain two of the other benefits from this feature instead of one.
### Natural Sorcerer

**Prerequisite:** Must not be a Heavenly Restriction.

Whether through your ancestry or through simply being born that way, your cursed energy reserves are naturally immense. You will add your Jujutsu Sorcerer level added twice + your proficiency bonus added twice when calculating your cursed energy maximum.
### Stamina Beast

**Prerequisite:** Must be a Heavenly Restriction.

Whether through training or through simply being born that way, your Stamina reserves are naturally immense. Your Stamina maximum increases by your Heavenly Restriction level + your proficiency bonus.
Your body, training, and instinct far surpasses what anyone else can accomplish. You gain additional Body Points equal to your equal to your proficiency bonus, and any time your proficiency bonus increases, you gain additional body points equal to that new value.

### Blessing of the Twin

**Prerequisite:** Human.

Your body has been blessed with an extraordinary transformation, granting you a second pair of arms, heightened senses, and a physique approaching the ideal form for sorcery.

Four Arms. You have two additional arms beneath your original pair. These arms are fully functional and can perform any task a normal arm or hand can perform. They can manipulate objects, open or close doors and containers, draw or stow objects, pick up or set down objects, and interact with your surroundings.

Your additional arms allow you to wield weapons as though you had four hands. You can wield up to four one-handed weapons simultaneously or up to two two-handed weapons simultaneously, provided you meet the normal requirements for wielding those weapons. Your additional arms do not grant you additional actions or attacks.

Twin-Weapon Fighting. You can engage in two-weapon fighting even when the weapons you wield lack the Light property. All other rules for two-weapon fighting still apply.

If you have the Dual Wielder feat, you can engage in two-weapon fighting with weapons that have the Heavy property, provided you meet the other requirements for wielding those weapons.

Rapid Loading. When you reload a weapon, reduce the action required to reload it by one step, following this progression: Action → Bonus Action → Reaction → No Action. You must have a free hand to reload a weapon.

Larger Form. Your size increases by one category, to a maximum of Large.

Enhanced Physique. Your Strength and Constitution scores each increase by 2.

Intimidating Presence. You have advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks.

Extra Eyes. Your additional eyes grant you advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
Independent Limbs. Your additional arms can function independently of your original arms, allowing them to manipulate objects and perform tasks while your primary arms are occupied. Your arms do not grant you additional actions, bonus actions, or reactions.

Additional Mouth. You have a second, fully functional mouth located on your torso. It can speak, eat, drink, and perform any other function a normal mouth can perform.

### Physically Gifted Heavenly Restriction

**Prerequisites:** Must be a human; must not possess the Cursed Energy Heavenly Restriction feat.

Heavenly Restriction Explained: This feat is one of multiple ways to use Heavenly Restriction. The most simple way is to use the Heavenly Restriction Class, which represents a character with extremely low cursed energy, and no way to manipulate it. The other ways to use it are with the Jujutsu Sorcerer Class, choosing the 25%, 50%, or Near Zero options. These represent a sorcerer who can manipulate cursed energy, but with a Heavenly Pact applied to them, trading cursed energy and even cursed technique for physical ability. Finally, you can choose the Zero Cursed Energy option with the Heavenly Restriction Class, representing the ultimate Heavenly Pact, fully free from cursed energy with the most powerful physical prowess.

Heavenly restrictions are binding vows placed on a sorcerer's body when they are born. This restriction limits your cursed energy, but in return, boosts your physical capabilities. There are 4 possible ways to use this background feat. You can give up 25% of your cursed energy maximum, 50% of your cursed energy maximum, almost all of your cursed energy, or leave it all behind..

#### 25%

This usage of the feat is only possible using the Jujutsu Sorcerer class. When you calculate your cursed energy maximum, you must first multiply it by .75 and then round up. In return, you gain a +1 to all physical ability scores and their maximums. You gain this benefit again at 11th level. Additionally, you gain 1 body feat, and you may use your Ability Score Improvement to increase your Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution scores up to their maximums.

#### 50%

This usage of the feat is only possible using the Jujutsu Sorcerer class. Your cursed energy amount will now be halved (rounded down). In return, you gain a +2 to all physical ability scores and their maximums. You gain this benefit again at 11th level. Additionally, you gain 1 Body Feats and you may use your Ability Score Improvement to increase your Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution scores up to their maximums.

#### Near Zero

Your Heavenly Restriction has traded off the majority of cursed energy and your cursed technique for physical prowess, though you are still a sorcerer capable of using cursed energy.

You do not have a Cursed Technique, and your cursed energy maximum will be equal to your Charisma modifier, and cannot be increased through use of feats or features that normally increase your cursed energy maximum. You also do not benefit from features relating to cursed energy that give you passive benefits, such as Cursed Empowered Strikes, Curse Enhanced Body, or Cursed Energy Reinforcement.

In return, you gain the following benefits:

You gain +2 to all physical ability scores and their maximums. You gain this benefit again at 11th level. You may use your Ability Score Improvement to increase your Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution scores up to their maximums.
You gain 1 Body Feats
You gain the Body Improvements feature from the Heavenly Restricted Class, and possess a number of Body Points equal to half of your level, rounded up. You don’t gain additional body points from any other source.
Your Martial Arts die instead follows the Heavenly Restriction class.
At 2nd level, you gain a number of Stamina Points equal to half of your level, rounded down plus your Constitution modifier. You don’t gain additional Stamina points from any other source.
At 2nd level, you gain a number of Battle Maneuvers equal to your proficiency bonus, using Stamina points to fuel them. You don’t gain additional maneuvers from any other source.
At 5th level, you gain the Heavenly Combatant feature from the Heavenly Restricted class, using Stamina points to fuel it.
#### Zero Cursed Energy

This usage of the feat is only possible using the Heavenly Restriction class. You are the pinnacle of Heavenly Restriction, completely free from cursed energy. You gain the following benefits:

The Restrained Body feature now gives you +2 at every score increase. The Semi-Awakened Body feature also gives you a +2 increase. The Awakened Body feature also gives you a +4 bonus or two +2 bonuses.
You gain 1 Body Feats of your choice that you meet the prerequisites for.
Your body, training, and instinct far surpasses what anyone else can accomplish. You gain additional Body Points equal to your proficiency bonus, and any time your proficiency bonus increases, you gain additional body points equal to that new value.
You can see cursed energy and things made of pure cursed energy such as barriers and creatures with the Invisible Force feature. You cannot be tracked by magical means. Abilities that sense ki, cursed energy, magic, or anything of the sort don't work for you. You also count as an object while inside a Domain Expansion, and cannot be detected by one while inside one (Ex: If you are trapped inside a domain that forces you to make a Constitution saving throw as its sure hit, you won't have to make the saving throw). You may also freely pass through barriers made of cursed energy of any kind, suffering no ill effects for doing so. When you pass through a barrier that presents an extra-dimensional space on the inside, such as a Domain Expansion, you do not appear in the manifested space, instead being in the space the barrier is in the real world, with no ill effects. As a bonus action, you can choose to enter or exit the space physically. If you choose to enter, you appear within the space like any other creature that would normally manifest inside it. When inside the Domain, you can exit the barrier freely at any time by physically exiting the barrier’s area.
### Blessed by the Sparks

**Prerequisite:** Must be a Jujutsu Sorcerer.

For mysterious and unknown reasons, you have been blessed by the sparks of the Black Flash. Upon getting the Black Flash cursed feat you can land them more reliably. As a bonus action, you can start concentrating, and while concentrating you can guarantee your next melee attack to trigger a black flash, upon which the concentration ends. If you take any damage, you need to succeed on a Constitution saving throw to maintain your concentration. The DC equals 10 or half the damage you take, whichever number is higher to maintain it. If you take damage from multiple sources, you make a separate saving throw for each source of damage. You can only concentrate like this if you are below half your maximum hit points while fighting an opponent with a CR or level higher than yours, or you have been participating in the combat for 5 rounds or more. If you are fighting an opponent with a CR or level of 5 or more higher than yours, you don't need to be at below half you maximum hit points. After concentrating like this, you can only begin to concentrate again after the end of your next turn.

The Charisma saving throw to land a Black Flash is reduced to 20. At 10th level, the DC is reduced to 15. At 18th level, you will always succeed this saving throw.

The DM may allow a character with this feat to start concentrating even without meeting any of the requirements stated if you deem it to be a narratively appropriate moment, and in the opposite fashion as well, with the moment not being fitting for a Black Flash as well.

Additionally, if you do not gain the Black Flash cursed feat by level five, then you automatically gain it. You also gain Personal Record upon landing your 4th Black Flash.

Taijutsu is the art of melee combat and martial arts, ranging from diverse techniques to make you a master of unarmed combat.

### Slam Master

**Prerequisite:** Strength 16 or higher.

You fight like a beast on the battlefield, focusing on throwing your opponents around. You gain the following Slams:

* **Brute Slam.** Once per round as part of an unarmed strike, you may make a grapple attempt against a creature. If you succeed, you may pick up the creature and slam it against a solid surface within 10 feet of you, such as the ground or a wall, dealing another roll of your martial arts die in damage. The surface takes twice as much damage as the creature. If it breaks, the creature takes another 2d6 damage of an appropriate type.
* **Heavy Slam.** Whenever you take the Attack action against a creature you are grappling, you may forgo an attack to slam the creature into the ground. Make a contested Strength (Athletics) check against the creature's Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. On a success, the creature is driven into the earth, your grapple ends, it takes bludgeoning damage equal to two rolls of your martial arts damage dice, and it is knocked prone. On a failure, the creature escapes and may move an additional 5 feet away as part of its successful check. If you roll a 20 on the Strength (Athletics) check and succeed, the creature takes bludgeoning damage equal to four rolls of your martial arts damage dice and has disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, skill checks, and Strength or Dexterity saving throws until the start of your next turn.

### Acrobatic Fighter

**Prerequisite:** Dexterity 16 or higher.

You have developed a quick, agile, and mobile fighting style, using your surroundings to enhance your movement and strikes.

* **Acrobatic Movement.** You can use your Dexterity score instead of your Strength score when determining the distance and height of your jumps. Your jump distance and jump height are doubled.
* **Leverage Strike.** When you make an unarmed strike within 5 feet of a solid, stationary surface or object other than the ground that can support or redirect your momentum, such as a wall, pillar, tree, railing, or large piece of furniture, you can use it to propel yourself into the attack. The unarmed strike deals one additional die of its normal damage.

If you are not within 5 feet of a suitable surface or object, you can instead use half your movement to drop to the ground and make a sweeping kick. Your unarmed strike deals the additional damage die provided by this feature.

* **Controlled Falls.** When you take damage from a fall, you take half as much damage. In addition, you are not knocked prone when you land from a fall.

### Cursed Martial Artist

**Prerequisites:** Cursed Strike enhancement at Advanced Tier; 8th level.

Your expertise with unarmed combat exceeds that of others at a similar skill level through complex training and mastery of martial practice.

* Your unarmed strikes when benefiting from the Cursed Strike enhancement have their critical hit range increased by 1. For example, if you would normally score a critical hit on a roll of 20, you score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 while using Cursed Strike. Additionally, whenever you score a critical hit with an unarmed strike, you can roll one additional unarmed strike damage die when determining the extra damage.
* If you have taken the Attack action on your turn and used only unarmed strikes, when you make an unarmed strike benefiting from Cursed Strike as a bonus action, the enhancement's cursed energy cost is reduced by 2, to a minimum of 1.
* As a reaction when a creature fails a melee attack roll against you, you can make one unarmed strike against it. On a successful hit, you may add your Charisma modifier, minimum +1, to the damage roll.

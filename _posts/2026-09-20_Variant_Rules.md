# Variant Rules

## Flanking

If a creature and at least one of its allies are adjacent to an enemy and on opposite sides or corners of the enemy's space, they flank that enemy, and each of them has advantage on melee attack rolls against that enemy.

## Realistic Knockback

The features within the Jujutsu Kaisen Supplement cause creatures to take damage according to how many feet they traveled before colliding with a structure. If you wish for these Knockback features to instead function more in line with actual physics, then use this rule.

Any features that deal knockback damage such as Colossal Physique, Heavy Finisher, Explosive Blow, or any other feature that deals damage based on colliding with structures, the creature will instead take damage based on how many feet they had left to travel before colliding with the structure.

For example, if a creature would be knocked back 120 feet by Heavy Finisher, and they collide with a structure after traveling 50 feet, then they would take a number of d12s equal to every 10 feet left to travel, which would be 70 feet.

## Intellect Over Will

With this rule, a Jujutsu Sorcerer may use their Intelligence rather than Charisma for the class (for their own features). The Jujutsu Sorcerer's saving throws become Strength or Dexterity and Intelligence, and their Cursed Energy Modifier becomes Intelligence, alongside any effects and features of theirs that would use Charisma being replaced with the use of Intelligence. This does not change the Near the Pinnacle feature.

All Cursed Feats that make reference to Charisma instead refer to Intelligence for a Jujutsu Sorcerer benefiting from this rule when they use them. This does not affect saving throw or check types; only abilities that specifically refer to the score in isolation are affected by this rule.

In addition, the following changes are made to the creature's features (if they possess them):

- **Cursed Enhanced Body Cursed Feat.** Whenever you make a Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw, you may spend Cursed Energy up to your Intelligence modifier as a reaction to add the number of energy spent as a bonus to the saving throw.
- **Self-Inflicted Saving Throws.** Charisma saving throws inflicted by your own features, such as from your Innate Technique or Sorcerer Path (for example, Vengeful Cursed Spirit or Divergent Fist), will be changed to Intelligence saving throws. This does not alter saving throws from effects that have the capacity to force other creatures to make the saving throw, such as an area of effect or radius that you have not omitted yourself from. This applies to rules or features that rely on saving throws that use Charisma related to sorcery that you make, such as Momentum Feats.
- **Soul Saving Throws.** Charisma saving throws and checks for Soul Learning will be changed to Intelligence saving throws and checks.

## Rhythm of the Sparks

When using this variant rule, all player characters have the ability to Black Flash as the combat ramps up. When a player character lands their 3rd or more natural 20 on an attack roll that qualifies for a Black Flash in an encounter, while equal to or below their level in HP, you can choose to make a DC 25 Charisma saving throw. On a failure, nothing happens. On a success, you will land a Black Flash, but you will not enter the zone. For the rest of the encounter, when they land a natural 20 on an attack roll that qualifies for a Black Flash, they will land a Black Flash.

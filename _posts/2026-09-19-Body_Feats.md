The body is one of the most important things for a sorcerer. Strength, durability, and speed can all be improved here.

### Athlete History

**Prerequisite:** Strength 14 or higher.

You were born unnaturally strong, capable of destroying walls through sheer brute force.

* Your Strength score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* Your unarmed strike damage die increases by 2 tiers (1 -> d4 -> d6 -> d8, and so on).
* Any attack roll you make using Strength deals twice as much damage to objects and structures.

### Colossal Physique

**Prerequisites:** Strength 16 or higher; Athlete History.

Your strength is capable of matching experts at cursed reinforcement or massive curse spirits and shikigami.

* Your Strength score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* You count as two sizes larger for grappling, shoving, disarming, and the weight you can carry, push, drag, or lift.
* Your unarmed strike damage die increases by 1 tier (d8 -> d10 -> d12, and so on).
* When you make a Strength check, you can choose to roll a 10 instead of rolling the d20. This can only be done to one roll if you have advantage and cannot be applied to a roll made with disadvantage.
* Once per round as part of a successful melee weapon attack, you can force the creature hit to make a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier). On a failure, it is knocked directly backward a number of feet equal to your Strength modifier times 10. If it collides with an unsecured object or structure, the creature and object take 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet traveled. If it collides with another creature, they both take damage equal to half your melee attack's damage roll, rounded down. On a critical hit, the creature has disadvantage on the saving throw and is knocked back twice as far.
* Whenever you make a Strength check or saving throw, you can give yourself advantage on the roll. You can do this a number of times equal to your Strength modifier, regaining all uses after a long rest. This may use Charisma if you have Cursed Energy Reinforcement.

### 50 Meters in 3 Seconds!

**Prerequisite:** Dexterity 14 or higher.

You have an innate speed advantage over others of your kind or have trained intensely in agility.

* Your Dexterity score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* Your base speed increases by 10 feet.
* You may add half your proficiency bonus, rounded down, to initiative rolls.

### Untraceable Speed

**Prerequisites:** Dexterity 16 or higher; 50 Meters in 3 Seconds!

Even by the supernatural standards of the Jujutsu world, your agility is fearsome.

* Your Dexterity score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* Your base speed increases by 10 feet.
* When you make a Dexterity check, you can choose to roll a 10 instead of rolling the d20. This can only be done to one roll if you have advantage and cannot be applied to disadvantage.
* You may perform the Dash action or make a skill check you are proficient in as a bonus action. While dashing, you may move along vertical surfaces and across liquids on your turn without falling during the move.
* As a reaction to passing a saving throw or an attack roll being made against you, you may move up to half your speed in a direction of your choice. This movement cannot be used to exit domains.

### Strong Body

**Prerequisite:** Constitution 14 or higher.

You are exceptionally durable, making you difficult to take down.

* Your Constitution score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* You will add an additional 1+half your proficenecy bonus (rounding down) to your Hitpoints.
* Reduce all damage you take except psychic damage by your Constitution modifier. Attacks using Cursed Energy or magic can bypass this reduction unless you are under the effects of Cursed Armor.

### Human Wall

**Prerequisites:** Constitution 16 or higher; Strong Body.

Your endurance is extraordinary, keeping you on the battlefield when others would fall.

* Your Constitution score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* Whenever you make a Constitution check or saving throw, you can give yourself advantage. You can do this a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier, regaining all uses after a long rest. This may use Charisma if you have Cursed Energy Reinforcement.
* Your hit point maximum increases by three times your level. Whenever you gain a level thereafter, you gain 3 additional hit points.
* As a reaction when reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can spend a Hit Die and regain hit points equal to the amount rolled. You can do this a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier, regaining all uses after a long rest. This may use Charisma if you have Cursed Energy Reinforcement.
* At the beginning of your turn, while suffering from exhaustion, you can enter a state in which you are unaffected by the detriments of the exhaustion condition. This lasts for a number of rounds equal to your Constitution modifier. Once used, you must complete a long rest before using it again.

### Resilient

**Prerequisite:** Constitution 14 or higher.

All your Hit Dice increase in size by one step. Your hit points increase by an amount equal to your level + 1, and future Hit Dice are treated as one size larger. You can take this feat multiple times, but you cannot increase a Hit Die above d12. For a Heavenly Restriction, the maximum is 2d8.

> **Design Note:** Calculate this as if you had the increased Hit Die from level 1. A 2d6 or 2d8 Hit Die is one Hit Die with an increased tier, not additional Hit Dice.

### Immense Stamina

**Prerequisites:** Constitution 14 or higher; Heavenly Restriction.

Your body has massive stamina reserves.

* Your Constitution score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* Add your Constitution modifier an additional time when determining your Stamina.

### Overflowing Stamina Reserves

**Prerequisites:** Constitution 16 or higher; Immense Stamina; Heavenly Restriction.

Your Stamina reserves are practically endless.

* Your Constitution score increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* Add your Constitution modifier an additional time when determining your Stamina.


### Improved Body Functions

**Prerequisites:** Cursed Spirit; Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution 15 or higher.

Your biology is an abnormality even by the standards of Cursed Spirits.

* One of your Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution scores increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* You gain four points to spend on options in the Body Functions List.

If you possess the Special Grade background feat or are immediately granted Special Grade upon becoming a Cursed Spirit through the A Curse Upon Death variant rule, the benefits are instead:

* One of your Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution scores increases by 2, or two of those scores increase by 1, to a maximum of 20.
* You gain five points to spend on options in the Body Functions List.

### Cursed Biology

**Prerequisites:** Cursed Spirit and 12th level, or Imaginary Vengeful Cursed Spirit.

Curses display strange and specialized biology, but you exhibit it on another level. Work with your DM to determine one unique bodily feature that lets you do something new or enhances something you already can do, generally related to something emblematic of your character or technique. For example, Dagon, a Womb, had a black orb in place of his stomach that allowed him to draw a sigil to expand a domain without hands and faster than normal.

If you are an Imaginary Vengeful Cursed Spirit without the Cursed Womb Template, these features become more powerful and draw on the concept from which you were born and your technique. You may have two features instead of one, as discussed with your DM: one based on a central power related to your concept and another enhancing your technique.

If you possess the Special Grade background feat, you may gain two features. If you are an Imaginary Vengeful Cursed Spirit with that feat and without the Cursed Womb Template, you may gain any number your DM allows, with a minimum of 3. These features must be centered around your concept or technique and may be extremely powerful.

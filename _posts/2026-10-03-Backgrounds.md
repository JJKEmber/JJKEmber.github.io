# Customizable Backgrounds

Your character's background represents the skills, training, and experiences they gained before becoming an adventurer. You may create a custom background by choosing the following benefits.

## Background Benefits

A custom background provides the following:

- **Skill Proficiencies:** Choose 2 skills.
- **Tool and Language Proficiencies:** Choose 2 tools and/or languages. You may choose two tools, two languages, or one of each.
- **Feat:** Choose 1 feat. You may choose either a Background Feat or a regular feat for which your character qualifies.
- **Starting Wealth:** You receive 50 gp from your background, in addition to the starting equipment provided by your class.

Your background does not provide additional starting equipment.
